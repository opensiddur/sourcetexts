# Printed page 89 (Hebrew; scan 114, leaf 113)

## THE MECHANISM THIS PAGE INTRODUCES: three columns, labelled by bare day-name

Ya'aleh v'Yavo's occasion word is set as THREE columns. Verified by cropping:

    LEFT            CENTRE          RIGHT
    Sukkoth         Pesaḥ           Rosh Ḥodesh        [English, italic]
    חַג הַסֻּכּוֹת      חַג הַמַּצּוֹת       רֹאשׁ הַחֹֽדֶשׁ

Hebrew reading order, so Rosh Hodesh is first (rightmost). A bare day-name over a
column IS an instruction — short for "say this on Rosh Hodesh" — and must be encoded as
one, not dropped as a label.

The word slots into the sentence: ...בְּיוֹם [column] הַזֶּה. — see the line after.
