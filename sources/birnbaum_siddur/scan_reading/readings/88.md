## THE TWO-COLUMN BLOCK, MIRRORED FOR ENGLISH READING ORDER

The Hebrew page 87 sets winter on the LEFT and summer on the RIGHT (Hebrew reads
right-to-left, so winter is read first, appearing at the *start* of the line). The
English page reverses the physical placement so the reading order stays the same:

    From Pesaḥ till December 4th say:         From December 4th till Pesaḥ say:
    Bestow a blessing                          Bestow dew and rain for a blessing

LEFT column here = summer (Pesach to Dec 4) = "Bestow a blessing"
RIGHT column here = winter (Dec 4 to Pesach) = "Bestow dew and rain for a blessing"

This is the mirror image of page 87's physical layout, but the READING ORDER is
identical: winter phrase corresponds to וְתֵן טַל וּמָטָר, summer to וְתֵן בְּרָכָה. Do not
encode column position; encode the season, which is what the headings state and what
carries the meaning.

## What matches and what doesn't, against page 87

- Refuah, Shanim (less the columns), Qibbutz Galuyot, Mishpat (with its asterisk
  substitution), Minim, and the opening of Tzadiqim are all present and in the same
  order.
- The asterisk substitution for Mishpat's seal matches page 87 exactly in mechanism:
  one asterisk in the running text, one rubric, one parenthesized alternative.
- Tzadiqim breaks at the same point as the Hebrew page — "who truly trust" ends the
  page, matching בְּשִׁמְךָ ending 87. Confirms the two pages are paginated together and
  the page-turn falls mid-sentence on both sides.
