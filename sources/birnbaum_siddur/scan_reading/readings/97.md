## Footnote apparatus

    הביננו, called מעין שמונה עשרה ("abstract of the Shemoneh Esreh"), was composed by
    Rabbi Samuel, one of the first generation of the Babylonian authors of the Talmud,
    who lived in the third century. This prayer (Berakhoth 29a) is a synopsis of the
    middle thirteen petitions of the Shemoneh Esreh, from אתה חונן until רצה. When
    recited on urgent occasions, הביננו is preceded by the opening three blessings and
    concluded by the last three blessings of the original Shemoneh Esreh.
