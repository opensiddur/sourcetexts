## Commentary, continued on printed 50

Catchword: `מזמור שיר`. The page ends after “could be”; the following is the
complete note read across the opening:

> מזמור שיר is a hymn of gratitude for recovery from a grave sickness. The psalmist
> relates that trouble came to him when he thought that he could be independent of
> God’s aid. In his distress he pleaded that his life might be spared, and his prayer
> was answered. He is determined to spend the rest of his life in thanksgiving.
