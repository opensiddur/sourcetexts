## Footnote apparatus (bottom of the page, below a rule)

One note, keyed `לעולם יהא`, saying the passage from there onward is the setting for the
Shema, and giving the fifth-century prohibition under Yezdejerd II as the reason the Shema
came to be embedded here rather than said plainly. It **runs across the opening** and
finishes at the foot of page 24.

This is his commentary at its most historical — not glossing a word but explaining why a
passage sits where it does.
