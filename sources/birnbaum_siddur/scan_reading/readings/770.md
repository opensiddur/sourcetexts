# Printed 770 — IA n794

## harachaman_festival

May the Merciful One grant us the day of unmixed happiness.

## harachaman_rh

May the Merciful One renew for us this year for happiness and blessedness.

## harachaman_sukkot

May the Merciful One restore for us the fallen Sukk’ah of David.

## harachaman_messiah

May the Merciful One grant us life in the days of the Messiah and in the world to come.

## magdil

He gives great victories to his chosen king, and shows kindness to his anointed prince, to David and his dynasty forever.

## grace_oseh

He who creates peace in his celestial heights, may he create peace for us and for all Israel; and say, Amen.

## grace_verses

Revere the Lord, you his holy ones; those who revere him suffer no want. Lions may be famishing and starving, but those who seek the Lord are not in want of any good thing. Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good; his mercy endures forever. Thou openest thy hand and satisfiest every living thing with favor. Blessed is the man who trusts in the Lord, ever relying on the Lord. I have been young and now I am old, but never have I seen the righteous man forsaken, nor his children begging bread. The Lord will give strength to his people; the Lord will bless his people with peace.

## commentary — harachaman_sukkot

סכת דוד הנופלת is taken from Amos 9:11 and connotes the ruined state of David.

## commentary — grace_verses

לא ראיתי צדיק נעזב that is, the poor and needy among the righteous are never completely abandoned. Sooner or later, righteousness is vindicated.

## source — magdil

Psalm 18:51.

## source — grace_verses

Psalms 34:10–11; 118:1; 145:16; Jeremiah 17:7; Psalms 37:25; 29:11.
