## Hodu through the paragraph before Ashrei

Source: https://archive.org/details/PhilipBirnbaumHaSiddurHaShalemTheDailyPrayerBook1949/page/n78/mode/1up

Read directly from the scan, then checked against the resolved Hebrew transcription. This installment starts at Hodu on 51–52 and ends at Yehi khevod on 57–58, before Ashrei and its notes. The Pesukei dezimrah wrapper starts at Hareni mezamen; Psalm 30 and Mourners’ Kaddish remain outside.

The turn from 51–52 falls inside I Chronicles 16:16, and the next turn inside Psalm 25:6.

The verse list at the foot of 58 omits Exodus 15:18, but its words in the body are identified as that verse. The preceding “יְיָ מֶלֶךְ, יְיָ מָלָךְ” is liturgical wording, not a complete biblical verse.
