# Birnbaum, printed page 746 (IA n770)

Read directly from the scan; comparison and corrections are preserved in `../lifecycle/`.

Gentlemen, let us say grace.

Blessed be the name of the Lord henceforth and forever.

We praise his name amidst the faithful;
May the Lord’s blessing rest upon you.

On behalf of the most revered God,
Mighty stronghold in times of distress,
The God who is girded with power,
The Lord majestic in high heaven—

We praise his name amidst the faithful;
May the Lord’s blessing rest upon you.

On behalf of the holy Torah,
Which is pure, unmistakably clear,
The Torah Moses bequeathed to us,
Moses, faithful servant of the Lord—

We praise his name amidst the faithful;
May the Lord’s blessing rest upon you.

On behalf of the priests, the Levites,
I call to the God of the Hebrews,
Singing his praise in all the far lands,
Blessing the Eternal at all times.

We praise his name amidst the faithful;
May the Lord’s blessing rest upon you.
