# Printed page 31 (Hebrew; scan 56, leaf 55)

Running head `ברכות השחר`, page number `31`. Opens and closes mid-passage: Pittum
ha-Ketoreth runs from page 29 through this page and onto page 33.

## The page

Four paragraphs of Pittum ha-Ketoreth — the eleven spices and their weights; Rabban Shimon
ben Gamliel; the baraitha of Rabbi Nathan; and Bar Kappara, cut at `מִפְּנֵי`.

No heading, no rubric, **no citation**, and **no footnote** — the second consecutive page
with a blank foot. The words are in `hebrew/31.txt`.

## The comparison

Eight differences over 206 words, all settled for the print, none open.

**Four are qamats qatan**, settled by rule.

**Two are the combined shin-dot/holam again** — `הַקֹּשְׁטְ` and `שְׁלֹשָׁה`, where the
edition drops the holam and keeps the shin dot, as it does in printed 29's `וּשְׁלשָׁה`.

**Two are a dagesh in the tsade of `הַצִּפֹּֽרֶן`**, and the page settles them itself. The
print sets the word three times. The edition points the first with a dagesh and the other
two without; the print carries it in all three, checked at 25× on the second — the dot
inside the tsade is the same size and weight as the unmistakable one in the pe beside it.
One witness inconsistent against one consistent is not a close call.
