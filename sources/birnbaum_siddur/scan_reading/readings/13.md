# Printed page 13 (Hebrew; scan 38, leaf 37)

Running head `ברכות השחר`, page number `13` at the top outer corner.

Opens mid-poem, finishing Yigdal from page 11, and ends cleanly at the close of a blessing
— the first page of this unit that does not break mid-sentence.

**No footnote apparatus.**

## The page

Yigdal's last eight lines, in the two-column setting page 11 describes, and then the
morning blessings begin.

Between the poem and the blessings, a centred Hebrew source citation:

    מסכת ברכות יא, א; ס, ב

Two references in one citation. **Comma between the folio and its side, semicolon between
the two references** — consistent with the comma convention on pages 5, 9 and 11, and the
first citation in this unit to carry more than one reference.

Then, in order: the blessing over washing the hands; אשר יצר; the blessing over occupying
oneself with words of Torah; והערב־נא, closing its own blessing; and אשר בחר בנו.

`וְהַעֲרֶב־נָא` is set with a maqqef — checked, after page 11 turned up a maqqef read where
the print has a space and page 9 one dropped where the print has it.

The words are in `hebrew/13.txt`.

## The comparison

Fourteen differences. Ten settled by rule and not carried to anyone: nine are the verse
lineation of Yigdal, which is the text rather than a formatting choice, and one is a
qamats qatan.

Four are in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, and one of them is not a point at all: the print reads
`לְקֵץ יָמִין` where the transcription has `לְקֵץ הַיָּמִין`, with the definite article. That
is a textual variant rather than an orthographic one, and it falls in the line of Yigdal
whose variant reading Birnbaum's own footnote discusses on page 11 — though the footnote is
about the fifth line and this is the twelfth, so they are not the same place.
