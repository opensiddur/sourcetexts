### Psalm 19

1. לַמְנַצֵּחַ, מִזְמוֹר לְדָוִד.

For the Choirmaster; a psalm of David.

2. הַשָּׁמַיִם מְסַפְּרִים כְּבוֹד אֵל, וּמַעֲשֵׂה יָדָיו מַגִּיד הָרָקִיעַ.

The heavens proclaim the glory of God; the sky declares his handiwork.

3. יוֹם לְיוֹם יַבִּיעַ אֹמֶר, וְלַיְלָה לְּלַיְלָה יְחַוֶּה דָּעַת.

Day unto day pours forth speech, and night unto night reveals knowledge.

4. אֵין אֹמֶר וְאֵין דְּבָרִים, בְּלִי נִשְׁמָע קוֹלָם.

There is no speech, there are no words; unheard is their voice.

5. בְּכָל הָאָרֶץ יָצָא קַוָּם, וּבִקְצֵה תֵבֵל מִלֵּיהֶם; לַשֶּׁמֶשׁ שָׂם אֹהֶל בָּהֶם.

Yet their message extends through all the earth, and their words reach the end of the world. In the heavens he has pitched a tent for the sun,

6. וְהוּא כְּחָתָן יֹצֵא מֵחֻפָּתוֹ, יָשִׂישׂ כְּגִבּוֹר לָרוּץ אֹרַח.

which is like a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, like an athlete rejoicing to run the course.

7. מִקְצֵה הַשָּׁמַיִם מוֹצָאוֹ, וּתְקוּפָתוֹ עַל קְצוֹתָם, וְאֵין נִסְתָּר מֵחַמָּתוֹ.

It sets out from one end of the heaven, and round it passes to the other end, and there is nothing hidden from its heat.

8. תּוֹרַת יְיָ תְּמִימָה, מְשִׁיבַת נָפֶשׁ; עֵדוּת יְיָ נֶאֱמָנָה, מַחְכִּימַת פֶּתִי.

The Lord’s Torah is perfect, refreshing the soul; the Lord’s testimony is trustworthy, teaching the simple man wisdom.

9. פִּקּוּדֵי יְיָ יְשָׁרִים, מְשַׂמְּחֵי לֵב; מִצְוַת יְיָ בָּרָה, מְאִירַת עֵינָיִם.

The Lord’s precepts are right, gladdening the heart; the Lord’s commandment is clear, enlightening the eyes.

10. יִרְאַת יְיָ טְהוֹרָה, עוֹמֶדֶת לָעַד; מִשְׁפְּטֵי יְיָ אֱמֶת, צָדְקוּ יַחְדָּו.

The Lord’s faith is pure, enduring forever; the Lord’s judgments are true, they are altogether just.

11. הַנֶּחֱמָדִים מִזָּהָב וּמִפַּז רָב, וּמְתוּקִים מִדְּבַשׁ וְנֹפֶת צוּפִים.

They are more desirable than gold, than much rare gold; sweeter are they than honey, than honey from the honeycomb.

12. גַּם עַבְדְּךָ נִזְהָר בָּהֶם, בְּשָׁמְרָם עֵקֶב רָב.

Thy servant is indeed careful with them; in keeping them there is great reward.

13. שְׁגִיאוֹת מִי יָבִין; מִנִּסְתָּרוֹת נַקֵּנִי.

Yet who discerns his own errors? Of unconscious faults hold me guiltless.

14. גַּם מִזֵּדִים חֲשֹׂךְ עַבְדֶּךָ, אַל יִמְשְׁלוּ בִי; אָז אֵיתָם, וְנִקֵּיתִי מִפֶּשַׁע רָב.

Restrain thy servant also from wilful sins; let them not have dominion over me; then shall I be blameless, and I shall be clear of great transgression.

15. יִהְיוּ לְרָצוֹן אִמְרֵי פִי וְהֶגְיוֹן לִבִּי לְפָנֶיךָ, יְיָ צוּרִי וְגוֹאֲלִי.

May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be pleasing before thee, O Lord, my Stronghold and my Redeemer.

### Psalm 34

1. לְדָוִד, בְּשַׁנּוֹתוֹ אֶת טַעְמוֹ לִפְנֵי אֲבִימֶלֶךְ, וַיְגָרְשֵׁהוּ וַיֵּלַךְ.

A song of David, when he feigned madness before Abimelech, who drove him out and he departed.

2. אֲבָרְכָה אֶת יְיָ בְּכָל עֵת; תָּמִיד תְּהִלָּתוֹ בְּפִי.

I bless the Lord at all times;|His praise is ever in my mouth.

3. בַּייָ תִּתְהַלֵּל נַפְשִׁי; יִשְׁמְעוּ עֲנָוִים וְיִשְׂמָחוּ.

My soul glories in the Lord;|The humble hear it and are glad.

4. גַּדְּלוּ לַייָ אִתִּי, וּנְרוֹמְמָה שְׁמוֹ יַחְדָּו.

Exalt the Lord with me,|And let us extol his name together.

5. דָּרַשְׁתִּי אֶת יְיָ וְעָנָנִי, וּמִכָּל מְגוּרוֹתַי הִצִּילָנִי.

I sought the Lord and he answered me,|And delivered me from all my fears.

6. הִבִּיטוּ אֵלָיו וְנָהָרוּ, וּפְנֵיהֶם אַל יֶחְפָּרוּ.

Those who look to him are jubilant,|And they are never abashed.

7. זֶה עָנִי קָרָא וַייָ שָׁמֵעַ, וּמִכָּל צָרוֹתָיו הוֹשִׁיעוֹ.

This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him;|He saved him from all his troubles.

8. חֹנֶה מַלְאַךְ יְיָ סָבִיב לִירֵאָיו וַיְחַלְּצֵם.

The angel of the Lord encamps|Around those who revere him, and rescues them.

9. טַעֲמוּ וּרְאוּ כִּי טוֹב יְיָ; אַשְׁרֵי הַגֶּבֶר יֶחֱסֶה בּוֹ.

Consider and see that the Lord is good;|Happy is the man who takes shelter with him.

10. יְראוּ אֶת יְיָ, קְדֹשָׁיו, כִּי אֵין מַחְסוֹר לִירֵאָיו.

Revere the Lord, you his holy people;|For those who revere him suffer no want.

11. כְּפִירִים רָשׁוּ וְרָעֵבוּ, וְדֹרְשֵׁי יְיָ לֹא יַחְסְרוּ כָל טוֹב.

Young lions may suffer want and hunger,|But those who seek the Lord shall lack nothing.

12. לְכוּ בָנִים, שִׁמְעוּ לִי, יִרְאַת יְיָ אֲלַמֶּדְכֶם.

Come, children, listen to me;|I will teach you how to revere the Lord.

13. מִי הָאִישׁ הֶחָפֵץ חַיִּים, אֹהֵב יָמִים לִרְאוֹת טוֹב.

Who is the man that desires life,|And loves a long life of happiness?

14. נְצֹר לְשׁוֹנְךָ מֵרָע, וּשְׂפָתֶיךָ מִדַּבֵּר מִרְמָה.

Keep your tongue from evil,|And your lips from speaking falsehood.

15. סוּר מֵרָע וַעֲשֵׂה טוֹב, בַּקֵּשׁ שָׁלוֹם וְרָדְפֵהוּ.

Shun evil and do good;|Seek peace and pursue it.

16. עֵינֵי יְיָ אֶל צַדִּיקִים, וְאָזְנָיו אֶל שַׁוְעָתָם.

The eyes of the Lord are toward the righteous,|And his ears are open to their cry.

17. פְּנֵי יְיָ בְּעֹשֵׂי רָע, לְהַכְרִית מֵאֶרֶץ זִכְרָם.

The Lord’s anger is set against evildoers,|To cut off their name from the earth.

18. צָעֲקוּ וַייָ שָׁמֵעַ, וּמִכָּל צָרוֹתָם הִצִּילָם.

When they cry, the Lord listens,|And delivers them from all their troubles.

19. קָרוֹב יְיָ לְנִשְׁבְּרֵי לֵב, וְאֶת דַּכְּאֵי רוּחַ יוֹשִׁיעַ.

The Lord is near to the broken-hearted,|And saves those who are crushed in spirit.

20. רַבּוֹת רָעוֹת צַדִּיק, וּמִכֻּלָּם יַצִּילֶנּוּ יְיָ.

A good man may have many ills,|But the Lord delivers him from them all.

21. {pb:309}שֹׁמֵר כָּל עַצְמוֹתָיו, אַחַת מֵהֵנָּה לֹא נִשְׁבָּרָה.

{pb:310}He protects all his limbs,|So that not one of them is broken.

22. תְּמוֹתֵת רָשָׁע רָעָה, וְשֹׂנְאֵי צַדִּיק יֶאְשָׁמוּ.

Evil destroys the wicked,|And those who hate the righteous are doomed.

23. פּוֹדֶה יְיָ נֶפֶשׁ עֲבָדָיו, וְלֹא יֶאְשְׁמוּ כָּל הַחֹסִים בּוֹ.

The Lord saves the life of his servants;|All who take shelter with him are never desolate.

### Psalm 90

1. תְּפִלָּה לְמֹשֶׁה, אִישׁ הָאֱלֹהִים. אֲדֹנָי, מָעוֹן אַתָּה הָיִיתָ לָּנוּ בְּדֹר וָדֹר.

A prayer of Moses the man of God. O Lord, thou hast been our shelter in every generation.

2. בְּטֶרֶם הָרִים יֻלָּדוּ, וַתְּחוֹלֵל אֶרֶץ וְתֵבֵל, וּמֵעוֹלָם עַד עוֹלָם אַתָּה אֵל.

Before the mountains were brought forth, before earth and world were formed—from eternity to eternity thou art God.

3. תָּשֵׁב אֱנוֹשׁ עַד דַּכָּא, וַתֹּאמֶר שׁוּבוּ בְנֵי אָדָם.

Thou turnest man back to dust, and sayest: “Return, you children of man.”

4. כִּי אֶלֶף שָׁנִים בְּעֵינֶיךָ כְּיוֹם אֶתְמוֹל כִּי יַעֲבֹר, וְאַשְׁמוּרָה בַלָּיְלָה.

Indeed, a thousand years in thy sight are like a day that passes, like a watch in the night.

5. זְרַמְתָּם, שֵׁנָה יִהְיוּ; בַּבֹּקֶר כֶּחָצִיר יַחֲלֹף.

Thou sweepest men away and they sleep; they are like grass that grows in the morning.

6. בַּבֹּקֶר יָצִיץ וְחָלָף, לָעֶרֶב יְמוֹלֵל וְיָבֵשׁ.

It flourishes and grows in the morning; in the evening it fades and withers.

7. כִּי כָלִינוּ בְאַפֶּךָ, וּבַחֲמָתְךָ נִבְהָלְנוּ.

For we are consumed by thy anger; by thy wrath we are hurried away.

8. שַׁתָּ עֲוֹנֹתֵינוּ לְנֶגְדֶּךָ, עֲלֻמֵנוּ לִמְאוֹר פָּנֶיךָ.

Thou settest our iniquities before thee, and our guilty secrets are exposed in the light of thy presence.

9. כִּי כָל יָמֵינוּ פָּנוּ בְעֶבְרָתֶךָ, כִּלִּינוּ שָׁנֵינוּ כְמוֹ הֶגֶה.

Indeed, all our days decline under thy displeasure; we spend our years like a fleeting sound.

10. יְמֵי שְׁנוֹתֵינוּ בָהֶם שִׁבְעִים שָׁנָה, וְאִם בִּגְבוּרֹת שְׁמוֹנִים שָׁנָה, וְרָהְבָּם עָמָל וָאָוֶן, כִּי גָז חִישׁ וַנָּעֻפָה.

The length of our life is seventy years, or by reason of strength, eighty years; their pride is only toil and futility; for it is speedily gone, and we fly away.

11. מִי יוֹדֵעַ עֹז אַפֶּךָ, וּכְיִרְאָתְךָ עֶבְרָתֶךָ.

Who knows the power of thy anger, to fear thee in proportion to thy displeasure?

12. לִמְנוֹת יָמֵינוּ כֵּן הוֹדַע, וְנָבִא לְבַב חָכְמָה.

Teach us how to number our days, that we may attain a heart of wisdom.

13. שׁוּבָה יְיָ, עַד מָתָי, וְהִנָּחֵם עַל עֲבָדֶיךָ.

Relent, O Lord; how long? Relent as to thy servants.

14. שַׂבְּעֵנוּ בַבֹּקֶר חַסְדֶּךָ, וּנְרַנְּנָה וְנִשְׂמְחָה בְּכָל יָמֵינוּ.

Satisfy us in the morning with thy kindness, that we may sing and rejoice throughout our days.

15. שַׂמְּחֵנוּ כִּימוֹת עִנִּיתָנוּ, שְׁנוֹת רָאִינוּ רָעָה.

Gladden us in proportion to the days wherein thou hast afflicted us, the years wherein we have seen evil.

16. יֵרָאֶה אֶל עֲבָדֶיךָ פָעֳלֶךָ, וַהֲדָרְךָ עַל בְּנֵיהֶם.

Let thy work be revealed to thy servants, and thy glory upon their children.

17. וִיהִי נֹעַם אֲדֹנָי אֱלֹהֵינוּ עָלֵינוּ, וּמַעֲשֵׂה יָדֵינוּ כּוֹנְנָה עָלֵינוּ, וּמַעֲשֵׂה יָדֵינוּ כּוֹנְנֵהוּ.

May thy favor, Lord our God, rest on us; establish for us the work of our hands; the work of our hands establish thou.

### Psalm 91

1. יֹשֵׁב בְּסֵתֶר עֶלְיוֹן, בְּצֵל שַׁדַּי יִתְלוֹנָן.

He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High abides under the protection of the Almighty.

2. אֹמַר לַייָ מַחְסִי וּמְצוּדָתִי, אֱלֹהַי אֶבְטַח בּוֹ.

I say of the Lord: “He is my refuge and my fortress, my God, in whom I trust.”

3. כִּי הוּא יַצִּילְךָ מִפַּח יָקוּשׁ, מִדֶּבֶר {pb:311}הַוּוֹת.

Indeed, he will save you from the snare of the fowler, and from the destructive {pb:312}pestilence.

4. בְּאֶבְרָתוֹ יָסֶךְ לָךְ, וְתַחַת כְּנָפָיו תֶּחְסֶה; צִנָּה וְסֹחֵרָה אֲמִתּוֹ.

With his pinions he will cover you, and under his wings you will find refuge; his faithfulness is a shield and buckler.

5. לֹא תִירָא מִפַּחַד לָיְלָה, מֵחֵץ יָעוּף יוֹמָם.

Fear not the terror of the night, nor the arrow that flies by day,

6. מִדֶּבֶר בָּאֹפֶל יַהֲלֹךְ, מִקֶּטֶב יָשׁוּד צָהֳרָיִם.

nor the pestilence that stalks in darkness, nor the destruction that ravages at noon.

7. יִפֹּל מִצִּדְּךָ אֶלֶף, וּרְבָבָה מִימִינֶךָ; אֵלֶיךָ לֹא יִגָּשׁ.

Though a thousand fall at your side, and a myriad at your right hand, it shall not come near you.

8. רַק בְּעֵינֶיךָ תַבִּיט, וְשִׁלֻּמַת רְשָׁעִים תִּרְאֶה.

Only with your eyes will you gaze, and see the reward of evil men.

9. כִּי אַתָּה, יְיָ, מַחְסִי; עֶלְיוֹן שַׂמְתָּ מְעוֹנֶךָ.

Thou, O Lord art my refuge! When you have made the Most High your shelter,

10. לֹא תְאֻנֶּה אֵלֶיךָ רָעָה, וְנֶגַע לֹא יִקְרַב בְּאָהֳלֶךָ.

no disaster shall befall you, no calamity shall come near your tent.

11. כִּי מַלְאָכָיו יְצַוֶּה לָּךְ, לִשְׁמָרְךָ בְּכָל דְּרָכֶיךָ.

For he will give his angels charge over you, to guard you in all your ways.

### elohei

אֱלֹהֵי הָרִאשׁוֹנִים וְהָאַחֲרוֹנִים, אֱלוֹהַּ כָּל בְּרִיּוֹת, אֲדוֹן כָּל תּוֹלָדוֹת, הַמְהֻלָּל בְּרֹב הַתִּשְׁבָּחוֹת, הַמְנַהֵג עוֹלָמוֹ בְּחֶסֶד וּבְרִיּוֹתָיו בְּרַחֲמִים. וַייָ לֹא יָנוּם וְלֹא יִישָׁן, הַמְעוֹרֵר יְשֵׁנִים, וְהַמֵּקִיץ נִרְדָּמִים, וְהַמֵּשִׂיחַ אִלְּמִים, וְהַמַּתִּיר אֲסוּרִים, וְהַסּוֹמֵךְ נוֹפְלִים, וְהַזּוֹקֵף כְּפוּפִים. לְךָ לְבַדְּךָ אֲנַחְנוּ מוֹדִים.

### ilu

Were our mouth filled with song as the sea [is with water], and our tongue with ringing praise as the roaring waves; were our lips full of adoration as the wide expanse of heaven, and our eyes sparkling like the sun or the moon; were our hands spread out in prayer as the eagles of the sky, and our feet as swift as the deer—we should still be unable to thank thee and bless thy name, Lord our God and God of our fathers, for one thousandth of the countless millions of favors which thou hast conferred on our fathers and on us. Thou hast delivered us from Egypt, Lord our God, and redeemed us from slavery. Thou hast nourished us in famine and provided us with plenty. Thou hast rescued us from the sword, made us escape the plague, and freed us from severe and lasting diseases. Until now thy mercy has helped us, and thy kindness has not abandoned us; mayest thou, Lord our God, never forsake us.

### al_ken

עַל כֵּן אֵבָרִים שֶׁפִּלַּגְתָּ {pb:333}בָּנוּ, וְרוּחַ וּנְשָׁמָה שֶׁנָּפַחְתָּ בְּאַפֵּינוּ, וְלָשׁוֹן אֲשֶׁר שַׂמְתָּ בְּפִינוּ, הֵן הֵם יוֹדוּ וִיבָרְכוּ, וִישַׁבְּחוּ וִיפָאֲרוּ, וִירוֹמְמוּ וְיַעֲרִיצוּ, וְיַקְדִּישׁוּ וְיַמְלִיכוּ אֶת שִׁמְךָ, מַלְכֵּנוּ. כִּי כָל פֶּה לְךָ יוֹדֶה, וְכָל לָשׁוֹן לְךָ תִשָּׁבַע, וְכָל בֶּרֶךְ לְךָ תִכְרַע, וְכָל קוֹמָה לְפָנֶיךָ תִשְׁתַּחֲוֶה, וְכָל לְבָבוֹת יִירָאוּךָ, וְכָל קֶרֶב וּכְלָיוֹת יְזַמְּרוּ לִשְׁמֶךָ, כַּדָּבָר שֶׁכָּתוּב: כָּל עַצְמוֹתַי תֹּאמַרְנָה, יְיָ מִי כָמוֹךָ, מַצִּיל עָנִי מֵחָזָק מִמֶּנּוּ, וְעָנִי וְאֶבְיוֹן מִגֹּזְלוֹ. מִי יִדְמֶה לָּךְ, וּמִי יִשְׁוֶה לָּךְ, וּמִי יַעֲרָךְ־לָךְ, הָאֵל הַגָּדוֹל, הַגִּבּוֹר וְהַנּוֹרָא, אֵל עֶלְיוֹן, קֹנֵה שָׁמַיִם וָאָרֶץ. {reader}נְהַלֶּלְךָ וּנְשַׁבֵּחֲךָ וּנְפָאֶרְךָ, וּנְבָרֵךְ אֶת שֵׁם קָדְשֶׁךָ, כָּאָמוּר: לְדָוִד, בָּרְכִי נַפְשִׁי אֶת יְיָ, וְכָל קְרָבַי אֶת שֵׁם קָדְשׁוֹ.

{pb:334}Therefore, the limbs which thou hast apportioned in us, the spirit and soul which thou hast breathed into our nostrils, and the tongue which thou hast placed in our mouth, shall all thank and bless, praise and glorify, extol and revere, hallow and do homage to thy name, our King. Indeed, every mouth shall praise thee; every tongue shall vow allegiance to thee; every knee shall bend to thee, and every person shall bow before thee. ## New commentary and source notes

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/19

 Psalm 19 has been summed up in the saying: “The starry sky above me and the moral law within me are two things which fill the soul with ever new and increasing admiration and reverence.”

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/34

בשנותו את טעמו (Psalm 34) refers to the incident related in I Samuel 21:11–16 where the Philistine king, to whom David fled for refuge, is called Achish. Finding himself recognized as the slayer of Goliath, David feigned madness, and so escaped vengeance. The psalm is arranged alphabetically, except that the verse beginning with the letter ו is omitted and there is an additional verse at the end.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/90

 Psalm 90 contrasts the eternity of God with the brevity of human life, and ends with a prayer for God’s forgiveness and favor.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/91

 Psalm 91 is termed שיר של פגעים, “a song against evil occurrences” (Shebuoth 15b). It describes the safety of those who trust in God amid the perils of their journey through life. ארך ימים is repeated so that the number of verses of this psalm reach a total of seventeen, the numerical value of טוב.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/135

 Psalm 135 is a hymn of praise particularly suitable for public worship, for it begins and ends with the liturgical Halleluyah. It is a mosaic of fragments from various biblical passages illustrating God’s greatness. The first verse, for example, is identical with Psalm 113:1, except that the clauses are transposed.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/136

 Psalm 136 is called in the Talmud Hallel ha-Gadol, “the Great Hallel” (Pesaḥim 118a) to distinguish it from the “Egyptian Hallel” (Psalms 113–118) sung on festivals. It differs from all other psalms in that each verse closes with a refrain, probably designed to be sung in full chorus by the people.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/33

 Psalm 33 is a hymn of praise called forth by some national deliverance. The opening call to praise is followed by a description of God’s righteous rule and creative omnipotence. He is to be praised for his choice and care of Israel, whose protection does not depend on military power but on God.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:prayer:nishmat

נשמת was well known in the talmudic period. A portion of this poem is quoted as part of the prayer for rain (Berakhoth 59b; Ta‘anith 6b). The phrase “countless millions of favors” probably refers to the drops of rain, each drop being a separate favor; indeed, the Talmud suggests that thanks should be given for every drop of rain. Nishmath is identified in the Talmud (Pesaḥim 118a) with ברכת השיר, recommended by the Mishnah for the closing of the Haggadah service on Passover.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:prayer:shokhen_ad

שוכן עד is borrowed from Isaiah 57:15. The initials of the four synonyms for “righteous” in בפי ישרים happen to form the acrostic יצחק; by re-arranging the verbs תתרומם, תתברך, תתקדש, תתהלל, the third letters spell רבקה. Such re-arrangement is found in the Sephardic Siddur.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/35/10

 Psalm 35:10.

### urn:x-opensiddur:text:bible:psalms/103/1

 Psalm 103:1.

## Encoding and collation decisions

- `urn:x-opensiddur:text:siddur:shabbat/shacharit` includes the shared preliminary morning service (Birkot Hashachar onward), Psalm 30, Mourners’ Kaddish, and Pesukei Dezimrah through Half Kaddish. It is a service wrapper that will grow as the subsequent pages are encoded.
- Its `pesukei_dezimra` child begins at Hareni Mezamen and excludes the preceding Mourners’ Kaddish. Nishmat and Half Kaddish are children of that section; Shochen Ad does not start a new service division.
- Shared prayers and chapters retain their established readings, with new repeat-printing provenance and occurrence page breaks. Psalm 100 is absent in this service.
- Seven new complete chapters own their biblical verse URNs. Earlier collected excerpts of their verses retain local milestone correspondences and biblical source attributes, preserving their own printed translations and surrounding text.
- Nishmat’s four parts have prayer namespace milestones. Ha’el Betaatzumot, Shochen Ad, Befi Yesharim and Uvmakhalot use the existing prayer namespace URNs. The quoted full verses Psalm 35:10 and 103:1 are biblical milestones; the quotation of Psalm 33:1 in Shochen Ad is a source-marked quotation because the full chapter already supplies its canonical address.
- Half Kaddish and the opening Mourners’ Kaddish have minyan gates in their callers. The service declares Shacharit, without forcing a calendar date or turning every occasion into a Sabbath.
- Printed full spellings וְגוֹאֲלִי (19:15), פּוֹדֶה (34:23), שֶׁעוֹמְדִים (135:2), and יְרוּשָׁלָיִם (135:21) are retained against Wikisource’s defective forms. Enlarged 311 confirms אוֹתֹת (135:9); 315 confirms מֵייָ (33:8). Psalm 90:8 uses ordinary holam on vav rather than the alternative Unicode holam-haser encoding.
- English Psalm 135:19 retains the scan’s duplicated “the the Lord”. The English does not translate the repeated Psalm 91:16; no translation has been invented.
