## What the two sides do differently here

- **The speaker label has a different shape on each side.** Page 85 sets "Reader" in
  small roman capitals, inline, before the Hebrew words. Page 86 sets "Reader:" as its
  own centred italic line, with a colon. Same instruction, two typographic shapes; each
  project carries the one its own readers saw.
- The asterisk mechanism, the parentheses, the short rule and the smaller type for
  Aneinu are all reproduced identically on the English side.
- The rubrics are word for word the same on both sides again.
