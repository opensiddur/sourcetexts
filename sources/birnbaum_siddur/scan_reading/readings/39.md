# Printed page 39 (Hebrew; scan 64, leaf 63)

Running head `ברכות השחר`, page number `39`. Opens and closes mid-chapter: Eizehu Mekoman
runs from page 37 through this page and onto page 41.

## The page

Mishnayoth `ד` through `ח` of Zevaḥim 5 — the burnt-offering; the communal peace-offerings
and guilt-offerings; the thanksgiving-offering and the nazirite's ram; the peace-offerings;
and the firstling, tithe and passover-offering, which the page turn cuts at `לְכָל`.

No heading, no rubric, no citation, no footnote. The words are in `hebrew/39.txt`.

## The comparison

**Fifteen differences over 167 words, and every one is qamats qatan** — settled by rule,
recorded by `compare --settle-qamats-qatan`, nothing needing the image at all. The second
such page in a row, and for the same reason page 37 was: this is Mishnah, and the two
witnesses are copying a text neither of them had to decide anything about.

The rule was applied by the tool rather than by hand, which is worth saying because the
tool refuses any difference it does not wholly cover. Fifteen found, fifteen recorded, none
declined — so every one of them really is the rule and not something wearing it.
