Blessed art thou, Lord our God, King of the universe. O God, thou art our Father, our King, our Creator, our Redeemer, the Holy One of Jacob, the Shepherd of Israel, the good King who doest good to all. Thou bestowest favors on us continuously; thou dost ever confer on us kindness and mercy, relief and deliverance, prosperity and blessing, life and peace and all goodness. Mayest thou never deprive us of any good thing.
