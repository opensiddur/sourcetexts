# Printed page 15 (Hebrew; scan 40, leaf 39)

Running head `ברכות השחר`, page number `15` at the top outer corner. Opens and closes
cleanly, like page 13.

## The page

The Priestly Blessing under its citation `במדבר ו, כד–כו`; Elu Devarim under
`פאה א, משנה א; מסכת שבת קכז, א`; Elohai Neshamah under `מסכת ברכות ס, ב`; then the first
two morning blessings.

**Three source citations on one page**, two of them carrying two references apiece, all
punctuated as the Hebrew side does throughout: comma between the reference and its
subdivision, semicolon between references.

The words are in `hebrew/15.txt`.

## Footnote apparatus: four short notes instead of one long one

A new shape. Pages 3, 5 and 7 carried one or two notes, each several sentences. This page
carries **four**, each a sentence or two, and all four gloss terms out of the *same*
passage — the list in Elu Devarim: פאה, בכורים, ראיון, גמילות חסדים.

None runs across the opening.

So the apparatus varies with what the page holds: a discursive note where he is explaining
a practice, a string of short glosses where he is defining the terms of a list. Encoding
does not change — each is a note keyed by a catchword — but a page of four short notes
targets four different URNs inside one passage, where the earlier pages targeted one.

## The comparison

Eight differences, seven settled without a question, and the three kinds are worth
separating because only one of them is about this book.

**Two are qamats qatan**, the transcription interpreting where this print has one glyph.

**Three are the word אָמֵן, which the transcription adds after the Priestly Blessing's
verses and the print does not carry.** A congregational response the Wikisource edition
supplies. Settled for the print without a crop: the reading has never once been wrong
about a consonantal skeleton in twelve hundred words, and a whole word present or absent
is exactly that.

**Two are a bracketed alternation** — the transcription offering a feminine form beside
the masculine in the last blessing, where the print sets one form only. That is the
Wikisource edition's editorial apparatus rather than a reading of the 1949 page, and it is
not a question about what Birnbaum printed.

One is left: a tsere against a shva, in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
