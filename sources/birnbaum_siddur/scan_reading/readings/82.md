## Rubric (italic, centred) — the same rubric as the Hebrew page

    The Shemoneh Esreh is recited in silent devotion while standing, facing east.
    The Reader repeats the Shemoneh Esreh aloud when a minyan holds service.

Word for word identical to the Hebrew page's. That is worth recording: his rubrics
are NOT always the same on the two sides, so where they are, it is a fact and not an
assumption.

Only ³ belongs to the Amidah; ¹ and ² are in the out-of-scope Geulah.

## Structure notes

- The heading SHEMONEH ESREH is English-side only. The Hebrew page has no heading at
  this point at all.
- Birnbaum names the nineteen benedictions himself, here. This is the source for the
  berakhah names, and it is his own list of the Talmud's designations.
- The commentary apparatus is set across the opening, not per page: a note may begin
  under the Hebrew page and finish under the English one.
