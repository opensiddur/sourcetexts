# Pirkei Avot: printed pages 477–534, Internet Archive n501–n558

The Hebrew, English translation, and English commentary were read directly from
all 58 scanned pages before comparison with the Wikisource transcription. The
page-keyed reading is `avot-477-534.json`; plain readings are in `hebrew/` and
`english/`. The `transcription/` slices were generated from Wikisource page files
and serve only as secondary proofreading evidence.

Keys such as `2.13` preserve Birnbaum’s printed paragraph numbers. A trailing `+`
continues the same paragraph across a page turn. `opening` and `closing` record
the repeated Kol Yisrael and Rabbi Hananya passages; `=` repeats the preceding
reading of the same formula. `6.intro` and `5.23prayer` are unnumbered paragraphs.
The notes field records target key, Hebrew catchword (or `cite`), and note text.
Inline `i` and `he` markers preserve italics and embedded Hebrew.

A blind second reader checked all pages. Subsequent comparison and enlarged crops
corrected consonants, vowels, dageshes, catchwords, and individually located stress
marks. The initial readings omitted many stress marks; this was a real defect,
not an allowed normalization. Applied corrections are recorded separately.
Print spellings prevail over the secondary text, including its final mem forms,
its pointing of מתקיימת, and its omitted phrases. Qamats qatan is normalized to
ordinary qamats because the print does not distinguish their glyphs. A combined
holam and shin dot is encoded as both logical marks.

Birnbaum’s numbering is not the standard Mishnah numbering. The importer maps
printed paragraphs onto canonical Mishnah milestones without changing paragraph
structure. In particular, printed 5.11 crosses the standard 5:8/5:9 boundary.
Chapter six is the conventional appended baraita collection; Birnbaum’s explanatory
note is retained. The opening citation remains “Sanhedrin 11:1” as printed, although
the address uses the alternate standard chapter numbering, Sanhedrin 10:1.

The seasonal instruction belongs to the siddur’s Avot transclusion. Weekly chapter
selection is editorial, with the schedule source documented in the importer’s
specification; Birnbaum himself supplies only the seasonal rubric. The complete
text remains independently addressable and can print every chapter without these
selection instructions.
