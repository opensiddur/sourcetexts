## Commentary, continued on printed 52

Catchword: `ברוך שאמר`. The page ends at “biblical se-”; the note continues as
“lections” on 52. Complete reading:

> ברוך שאמר is composed of eighty-seven words, a number suggesting the numerical
> value of פז (“refined gold”). This hymn introduces the biblical selections entitled
> פסוקי דזמרה (“verses of praise”). It is included in the ninth century *Siddur*
> of Amram Gaon.
