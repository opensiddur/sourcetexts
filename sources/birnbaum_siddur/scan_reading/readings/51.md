Source: https://archive.org/details/PhilipBirnbaumHaSiddurHaShalemTheDailyPrayerBook1949/page/n75/mode/1up

Running head: `תפלת שחרית`; page number 51. These are page furniture, not a new heading.
The requested installment stops at `בַּתִּשְׁבָּחוֹת.` before the Chronicles citation
and Hodu. `hebrew/51.txt` is therefore a partial-page reading, explicitly ending there.

The page opens with Kaddish’s last two paragraphs. They differ from Kaddish d’Rabbanan:
`וְחַיִּים` has no `טוֹבִים`, and `הוּא יַעֲשֶׂה` has no `בְּרַחֲמָיו`.
These are independent readings, not transclusions of the earlier ending.

`הֲרֵינִי מְזַמֵּן אֶת פִּי לְהוֹדוֹת וּלְהַלֵּל וּלְשַׁבֵּֽחַ אֶת בּוֹרְאִי.`
is set in small Hebrew above Barukh sheamar. It is spoken text, not an editorial
instruction. The facing English page does not translate it.

Barukh sheamar is one paragraph, with an inline `Reader` immediately before
`יָחִיד, חֵי הָעוֹלָמִים`. The encoding parts there for alignment.
The print has a stress mark in `מֶֽלֶךְ` and a maqqef in `עֲדֵי־עַד`.

## Commentary, continued on printed 52

Catchword: `ברוך שאמר`. The page ends at “biblical se-”; the note continues as
“lections” on 52. Complete reading:

> ברוך שאמר is composed of eighty-seven words, a number suggesting the numerical
> value of פז (“refined gold”). This hymn introduces the biblical selections entitled
> פסוקי דזמרה (“verses of praise”). It is included in the ninth century *Siddur*
> of Amram Gaon.
