## What the two sides do differently, and it is not accidental

- **The heading KEDUSHAH is English-side only.** The Hebrew page 83 sets the rubric
  ("When the Reader repeats...") at that point and no heading at all. Same as
  SHEMONEH ESREH on 82. Both of Birnbaum's two headings in this whole unit are on the
  English side.
- **The speaker labels are Hebrew-side only.** Page 83 sets "Reader" in small roman
  capitals before לְעֻמָּתָם and before וּבְדִבְרֵי; page 84 has no label at either point
  and simply translates the words ("Those opposite them say: Blessed—").
- **The parentheses around the conditional inserts are on both sides.**
- **The rubrics are word for word the same on both sides on this opening.**
- Scripture verses are set as indented display lines on the English side; the Hebrew
  side sets them as ordinary centred lines.
