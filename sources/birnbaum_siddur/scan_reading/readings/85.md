## THE MECHANISM THIS PAGE INTRODUCES: the asterisk substitution

Birnbaum marks a *substitutable* passage with an asterisk in the running text and
repeats the asterisk before the rubric that governs it. It appears twice on this page,
both times on the seal of Kedushat ha-Shem, and the rubric says **substitute**, not
**add**:

    …אָֽתָּה. * בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, יְיָ, הָאֵל הַקָּדוֹשׁ.
    *Between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur substitute:
    (בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, יְיָ, הַמֶּֽלֶךְ הַקָּדוֹשׁ.)

So the two seals are alternatives: on the Ten Days הָאֵל הַקָּדוֹשׁ is NOT said and
הַמֶּֽלֶךְ הַקָּדוֹשׁ is said in its place. This is a substitution, not an insertion, and it
must not be encoded as an unconditional seal plus a conditional extra.
