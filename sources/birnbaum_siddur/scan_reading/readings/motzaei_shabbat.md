## Conditions

Birnbaum omits Vihi Noam and V’atah Kadosh when a festival occurs during the
coming week. The user clarified that Erev Pesach alone does not cause omission; Pesach must fall within Sunday–Friday. The encoded
calendar rule examines Sunday–Friday, respecting Israel/diaspora festival days.
The separate Tishah B’Av rubric omits Vihi Noam and Psalm 91, but not V’atah Kadosh.
Birkat Levanah is an independent unit; its visibility instruction does not restrict
it to Saturday night. No additional moon-age cutoff is supplied by these pages.

the English on 542 reads “whole household of Israel.” Both differ from the
