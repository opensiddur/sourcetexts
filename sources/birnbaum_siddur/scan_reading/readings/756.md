# Printed 756 — IA n780

## yotzer_haadam

Blessed art thou, Lord our God, King of the universe, Creator of men.

## asher_yatzar_haadam

Blessed art thou, Lord our God, King of the universe, who hast created man in thy image, and didst forever form woman out of his frame to be beside him. Blessed art thou, O Lord, Creator of man.

## sos_tasis

May Zion exult at the joyful reunion of her children in Jerusalem. Blessed art thou, O Lord, who causest Zion to rejoice in her children.

## sameach_tesamach

O give abundant joy to these loved companions, even as thou didst gladden thy creation of old in the Garden of Eden. Blessed art thou, O Lord, who givest joy to groom and bride.

## asher_bara

Blessed art thou, O Lord, King of the universe, who hast created groom and bride, joy and gladness, delight and cheer, love and harmony, peace and companionship. Lord our God, may there soon be heard in the cities of Judah, in the streets of Jerusalem, the sound of joy and gladness, the sound of joyous wedding celebrations, the sound of young people feasting and singing. Blessed art thou, O Lord, who makest the groom rejoice with the bride.

## devai_haser

Banish grief and anguish;
Let the speechless exult.
Guide us in the righteous paths;
Heed the blessings of priests.

## wedding_zimmun

With your permission, gentlemen, let us now bless our God in whose abode is joy and of whose food we have eaten.

## wedding_response

Blessed be our God in whose abode is joy, of whose food we have eaten and through whose goodness we live.

## commentary — devai_haser

דוי הסר is a poem by Dunash ben Labrat, the renowned poet and grammarian of the tenth century. His name דונש forms the acrostic of the four lines.
