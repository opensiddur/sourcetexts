## THE MECHANISM THIS PAGE INTRODUCES: two columns side by side

Birkat ha-Shanim's seasonal phrase is set as TWO COLUMNS, each with its own heading.
Verified by cropping the block, because the geometry is easy to get backwards:

    LEFT of the page                        RIGHT of the page
    From December 4th till Pesaḥ say:       From Pesaḥ till December 4th say:
    וְתֵן טַל וּמָטָר לִבְרָכָה                        וְתֵן בְּרָכָה

So the left column is the WINTER phrase and the right column the SUMMER one. The two
are exclusive alternatives, not an insertion: one phrase or the other is said, and the
sentence continues below in a single shared line.

*** READING ORDER IS RIGHT COLUMN FIRST, SO THE SUMMER PHRASE COMES FIRST. *** The page
is RTL and the rightmost column is read first — the same rule that puts רֹאשׁ הַחֹֽדֶשׁ first
among Ya'aleh v'Yavo's three columns on page 89. The facing English page 88 confirms it
independently: it mirrors the placement so that "Bestow a blessing" is leftmost and is
therefore read first there too. Both sides read summer, then winter.

*** THIS IS THE DIASPORA RULE, STATED AS A CIVIL DATE. *** Birnbaum gives December 4th,
not 7 Marcheshvan — the `tal-umatar` feature, and the one place the liturgical year
consults the civil calendar. He gives no Eretz Yisrael date at all.
