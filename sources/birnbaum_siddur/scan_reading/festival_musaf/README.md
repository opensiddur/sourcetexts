# Festival Musaf through the prayer for dew

Internet Archive n633–n660 corresponds to printed609–636 (scan pages634–661 in pages.json). These readings were made directly from the images before consulting the Wikisource transcription or existing XML. Independent Hebrew readers covered609–625 and627–635; a separate reader recorded the English and all rubrics and notes.

`aligned-readings.json` is the final bilingual reading used by the importer. `reading-N.txt` extracts the Hebrew in printed-page order, including page continuations and the priestly blessing’s word cues. The original primary and second-reader artifacts are retained as evidence of corrections, not as alternative final texts. `reviewed-hebrew.json` is an intermediate adjudication snapshot; the aligned reading supersedes it.

The fourteen `mechanical-N.txt` files come from `transcription.page_slice` of the page wikitext, not hand-built selections. All spans resolve. The second-half loader explicitly maps the external `עשרת הדברות/ניקוד` page, whose basename would otherwise collide with a foundation page. Raw differences are retained, including printed repeated Sukkot offering paragraphs, the mechanical transcription’s unprinted Israel-only additions to Ve-te‘erav, and different placement of the priestly response columns. `final-raw-comparison.json` is an audit diff, not a claim that these differently structured witnesses are identical.

Corrections checked against enlarged print include:609 גִבּוֹר and הַגָּֽשֶׁם;613/615 הַשָּׁבֻעוֹת;615/623 עַל יְדֵי;617 עֹלָה;621 וּבַשְּׁבִיעִי;623 לִשְׁמוֹר and נוֹדֶה לְךָ;625 וְכֹל, וְגוֹאֲלִי and קַדְמוֹנִיּוֹת;629 וְחָנֵּֽנִי with meteg and אֲבָרְכֵם without it;631 אַף, סַבְרִי and no ורבנן;633 רֹב and the feminine rhyme אַרְצָךְ;635 שִׂימָה and בְּמַאֲדִירֶֽיהָ. Initial Tal readings also misread consonants and several vowels; enlarged lines corrected those readings before authoring. The first-half second reader omitted many metegs: those omissions were not treated as evidence to remove the primary reading’s marks.

The printed619 commentary literally cites “Numbers29:41–61”; this apparent printing error is retained. English omissions remain omissions: no supplied translation for Savri or the extra Hebrew dream categories. Tal’s printed poetic lineation is preserved. The single printed shin/holam dot is represented by both logical Unicode marks, following project policy.

Functional interpretation confirmed by the editor: longer festival Kedushah on Shabbat Chol Hamoed; the shorter form on weekday Chol Hamoed; optional repetition of Uminchatam after each Sukkot day paragraph has its own custom setting and defaults to MAYBE. Israel uses the actual day’s Sukkot offering; diaspora Chol Hamoed uses the printed paired days. Whether Kohanim actually bless the congregation is a separate MAYBE setting, additionally requiring a minyan and the Reader’s repetition.
