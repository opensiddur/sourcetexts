PUTTING  ON  THE  TALLITH Before  putting  on  the  taUith:
Bless  the  Lord,  O   my  soul!  Lord  my  God,  thou  art  very  great; thou  art  robed  in  glory  and  majesty.  Thou  wrappest  thyself  in  light as  in  a   garment;  thou  spreadest  the  heavens  like  a   curtain.2
I   am  enwrapping  myself  in  the  fringed  garment  in  order  to fulfill  the  command  of  my  Creator,  as  it  is  written  in  the  Torah: “They  shall  make  fringes  for  themselves  on  the  corners  of  tlioir garments  throughout  their  generations.”3  Even  as  I   cover  myself with  the  tallith  in  this  world,  so  may  my  soul  deserve  to  be  robed  in a   beautiful  garment  in  the  world  to  come,  in  Paradise.  Amen.
