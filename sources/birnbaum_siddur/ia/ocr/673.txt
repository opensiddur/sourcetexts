PRAYER  FOR  DEW
Chanted  on  the  first  day  of  Pesah  during  Musaf Reader:
Blessed  art  thou,  Lord  our  God  and  God  of  our  fathers,  God  of Abraham,  God  of  Isaac  and  God  of  Jacob;  great,  mighty  and  re- vered God,  sublime  God,  who  bestowest  lovingkindness,  and  art Master  of  all  things;  who  rememberest  the  good  deeds  of  our fathers,  and  who  wilt  graciously  bring  a   redeemer  to  their  children’s children  for  the  sake  of  thy  name.  O   King,  Supporter,  Savior  and Shield!
With  thy  approval  I   utter  deep  thoughts,
To  cheer  thy  people  by  this  prayer  for  dew.
Dew  refreshes  the  earth  and  its  verdure,
Joyous  to  be  under  thy  protection.
May  dew — symbol  of  youth — shield  our  young.
