A   Pilgrim  Song.  O   Lord,  remember  David  and  all  his  affliction; how  he  swore  to  the  Lord,  and  vowed  to  the  Mighty  One  of Jacob:  “I  will  not  enter  my  house,  I   will  not  lie  on  my  bed,  I wall  not  give  sleep  to  my  eyes,  nor  slumber  to  my  eyelids,  until  I find  a   place  for  the  Lord,  a   residence  for  the  Mighty  One  of  Jacob.” We  heard  of  the  ark  in  Ephrath;  we  found  it  in  the  fields  of  Yaar. Let  us  enter  his  dwelling;  let  us  worship  at  his  footstool.  Arise,  O Lord,  to  thy  resting-place,  thou  and  thy  glorious  ark.
