Who  knows  the  power  of  thy  anger,  to  fear  thee  in  proportion  to  thy displeasure?  Teach  us  how  to  number  our  days,  that  we  may  attain a   heart  of  wisdom.  Relent,  O   Lord ;   how  long?  Relent  as  to  thy  ser- vants. Satisfy  us  in  the  morning  with  thy  kindness,  that  we  may sing  and  rejoice  throughout  our  days.  Gladden  us  in  proportion  to the  days  wherein  thou  hast  afflicted  us,  the  years  wherein  we  have seen  evil.  Let  thy  work  be  revealed  to  thy  servants,  and  thy glory  upon  their  children.  May  thy  favor,  Lord  our  God,  rest on  us;  establish  for  us  the  work  of  our  hands;  the  work  of  our hands  establish  thou.
