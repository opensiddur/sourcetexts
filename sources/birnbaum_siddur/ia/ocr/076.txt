Lord  my  God,  I   cried  to  thee,  and  thou didst  heal  me.  0   Lord,  thou  hast  lifted  me  up  from  the  grave; thou  hast  let  me  live,  that  I   should  not  go  down  to  the  pit.  Sing to  the  Lord,  you  who  are  godly,  and  give  thanks  to  his  holy  name. For  his  anger  only  lasts  a   moment,  but  his  favor  lasts  a   lifetime; weeping  may  lodge  with  us  at  evening,  but  in  the  morning  there are  shouts  of  joy.  I   thought  in  my  security  I   never  would  be  shaken. O   Lord,  by  thy  favor  thou  hadst  established  my  mountain  as  a stronghold;  but  when  thy  favor  was  withdrawn,  I   was  dismayed. To  thee,  0   Lord,  I   called;  I   appealed  to  my  God:  “What  profit would  my  blood  be,  if  I   went  down  to  the  grave?  Will  the  dust praise  thee?  Will  it  declare  thy  faithfulness?  Hear,  0   Lord,  and be  gracious  to  me;  Lord,  be  thou  my  helper.”  Thou  hast  changed my  mourning  into  dancing;  thou  hast  put  off  my  sackcloth  and girded  me  with  joy;  so  that  my  soul  may  praise  thee,  and  not  be silent.  Lord  my  God,  I   will  thank  thee  forever.
