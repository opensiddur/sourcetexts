By  the  seventh  day  God  had  completed  his  work  which  lie had  made,  and  he  rested  on  the  seventh  day  from  all  his  work  in which  he  had  been  engaged.  Then  God  blessed  the  seventh  day and  hallowed  it,  because  on  it  he  rested  from  all  his  work  which he  had  created.3
