Psalm  33
Rejoice  in  the  Lord,  you  righteous;  for  the  upright  it  is  fitting to  give  praise.  Give  thanks  to  the  Lord  with  harp;  sing  his  praises with  the  ten-stringed  lute.  Sing  to  him  a   new  song;  play  skilfully amid  shouts  of  joy.  The  word  of  the  Lord  is  right;  all  his  work  is done  with  faithfulness.  He  loves  righteousness  and  justice;  the earth  is  full  of  the  Lord’s  kindness.  By  the  word  of  the  Lord  the heavens  were  made,  and  all  their  host  by  the  breath  of  his  mouth. He  gathers  the  waters  of  the  sea  as  a   heap;  he  lays  up  the  deeps in  storehouses.  Let  the  earth  revere  the  Lord;  let  all  the  inhabi- tants of  the  world  stand  in  awe  of  him.  For  he  but  spoke,  and  it came  into  being;  he  but  commanded,  and  it  stood  forth.  The  Lord annuls  the  counsel  of  nations;  he  frustrates  the  designs  of  peoples.
