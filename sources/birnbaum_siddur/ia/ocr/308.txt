We  bend  the  knee  and  bow  and  acknowledge; before  the  supreme  King  of  kings,  the  Holy  One,  blessed  be  he, that  it  is  he  who  stretched  forth  the  heavens  and  founded  the earth.  His  seat  of  glory  is  in  the  heavens  above;  his  abode  of majesty  is  in  the  lofty  heights.  He  is  our  God,  there  is  none  else; truly,  he  is  our  King,  there  is  none  besides  him,  as  it  is  written  in his  Torah:  “You  shall  know  this  day,  and  reflect  in  your  heart, that  it  is  the  Lord  who  is  God  in  the  heavens  above  and  on  the earth  beneath,  there  is  none  else.”1
