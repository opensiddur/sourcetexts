Thou  didst  form  thy  world  long  ago,  completing  thy  work  on the  seventh  day.  Thou  hast  loved,  favored  and  exalted  us  above all  nations,  and  sanctified  us  with  thy  commandments.  Thou,  our King,  hast  brought  us  near  to  thy  service,  and  called  us  by  thy great  and  holy  name.  Thou,  Lord  our  God,  hast  graciously  given us  Sabbaths  for  rest,  and  new  moon  festivals  for  atonement.  But since  we  and  our  fathers  sinned  against  thee,  our  city  is  destroyed, our  sanctuary  is  laid  waste,  our  glory  is  departed,  and  removed  is the  glory  from  the  source  of  our  life.  We  cannot  perform  our  duties in  thy  chosen  House,  the  great  and  holy  Temple  which  was  called by  thy  name,  on  account  of  the  destruction  that  has  come  upon thy  sanctuary.  May  it  be  thy  will,  Lord  our  God  and  God  of  our fathers,  to  bring  us  in  joy  back  to  our  land  and  to  plant  us  within our  borders.
