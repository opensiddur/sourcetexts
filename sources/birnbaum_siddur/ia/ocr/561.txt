4.  This  is  the  way  of  Torah  study:  eat  bread  with  salt,  drink water  by  measure,  sleep  on  the  bare  ground,  and  live  a   life  of  hard- ship while  you  toil  in  the  Torah  [study  of  the  Torah  is  expected even  if  one  is  extremely  poor].  If  you  do  this,  “happy  shall  you  be and  it  shall  be  well  with  you”;3  happy  shall  you  be  in  this  world, and  it  shall  be  well  with  you  in  the  world  to  come.
5.  Do  not  seek  greatness  for  yourself  and  do  not  crave  honor; let  your  deeds  exceed  your  learning;  do  not  desire  the  table  of  kings, for  your  table  is  greater  than  theirs,  your  crown  is  more  glorious than  theirs;  your  Employer  can  be  trusted  to  pay  you  for  your work.
