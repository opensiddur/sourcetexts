MOURNERS’  KADDISH
Recited,  after  the  burial
Glorified  and  sanctified  be  God’s  great  name  throughout  the world  which  he  will  renew,  reviving  the  dead  and  raising  them  to life  eternal;  rebuilding  the  city  of  Jerusalem  and  establishing  his shrine  therein;  uprooting  idolatry  from  the  earth  and  restoring divine  worship  to  its  site.  May  the  Holy  One,  blessed  be  he,  reign in  his  majestic  glory  in  your  lifetime  and  during  your  days,  and within  the  life  of  the  entire  house  of  Israel,  speedily  and  soon; and  say,  Amen.
