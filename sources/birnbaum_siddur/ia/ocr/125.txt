ABRIDGED  SHEMONEH  ESREH Used  when  one  is  unable  to  recite  the  complete  Amidah
Grant  us,  Lord  our  God,  wisdom  to  learn  thy  ways;  subject our  heart  to  thy  worship;  forgive  us  so  that  we  may  be  redeemed; keep  us  from  suffering;  satisfy  us  with  the  products  of  thy  earth; gather  our  dispersed  people  from  the  four  corners  of  the  earth. Judge  those  who  stray  from  thy  faith;  punish  the  wicked;  may  the righteous  rejoice  over  the  rebuilding  of  thy  city,  the  reconstruction of  thy  Temple,  the  flourishing  dynasty  of  thy  servant  David and  the  continuance  of  the  offspring  of  thy  anointed,  the  son  of Jesse.  Answer  us  before  we  call.  Blessed  art  thou,  0   Lord,  who hearest  prayer.
Between  Rosh  Hashanah  and  Yom  Kippur  and  on  fast  days:
Our  Father,  our  King,  we  have  sinned  before  thee.
Our  Father,  our  King,  we  have  no  king  except  thee.
Our  Father,  our  King,  deal  with  us  kindly  for  the  sake  of  thy
name.
From  Rosh  Hashanah  to  Yom  Kippur: Our  Father,  our  King, renew  for  us  a   good  year.
On  fast  days:
Our  Father,  our  King, bestow  on  us  a   good  year.
Our  Father,  our  King,  abolish  all  evil  decrees  against  us.
