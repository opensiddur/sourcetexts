HYMN  OF  GLORY Recited  in  responsive  form The  ark  is  opened.
I   sing  hymns  and  compose  songs Because  my  soul  longs  for  thee.
My  soul  desires  thy  shelter,
To  know  all  thy  mystery.
When  I   speak  of  thy  glory,
My  heart  yearns  after  thy  love.
Hence  I   utter  thy  glories,
And  offer  thee  songs  of  love.
I   tell  thy  praise,  though  I   have  not  seen  thee;
I   describe  thee,  though  I   have  not  known  thee.
Through  thy  prophets  amidst  thy  worshipers Didst  thou  show  forth  thy  majestic  splendor.
