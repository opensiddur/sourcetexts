Ethics  of  the  Fathers
28.  Rabbi  Elazar  ha-Kappar  said:  Envy,  lust  and  ambition shorten  a   man’s  life.
29.  He  used  to  say:  Those  who  are  born  are  destined  to  die; those  who  are  dead  are  destined  to  be  brought  to  life  again;  and the  living  are  destined  to  be  judged.  [It  is  for  you]  to  know, to  proclaim,  to  be  convinced  that  he  is  God,  he  the  Maker,  he  the Creator,  he  the  Discerner,  he  the  Judge,  he  the  Witness,  he  the Complainant;  it  is  he  who  will  judge.  Blessed  be  he  in  whose  pre- sence there  is  no  wrongdoing,  nor  forgetting,  nor  partiality,  nor taking  of  bribes.  Know  that  all  is  according  to  reckoning,  and  let not  your  imagination  persuade  you  that  the  grave  is  a   place  of refuge  for  you.
