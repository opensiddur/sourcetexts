For  everything,  Lord  our  God,  we  thank  thee  and  bless  thee — be  thy  name  forever  blessed  by  all — as  it  is  written:  “When  you have  eaten  and  are  satisfied,  you  shall  bless  the  Lord  your  God for  the  good  land  he  has  given  you.”1  Blessed  art  thou,  0   Lord, for  the  land  and  the  sustenance.
Have  mercy,  Lord  our  God,  on  Israel  thy  people,  on  Jerusalem thy  city,  on  Zion  the  abode  of  thy  majesty,  on  the  royal  house  of David  thy  chosen  one,  and  on  the  great  and  holy  Temple  that bears  thy  name.  Our  God,  our  Father,  tend  and  nourish  us;  sus- tain and  maintain  us;  grant  us  deliverance.  Speedily,  Lord  our God,  grant  us  relief  from  all  our  troubles.  Lord  our  God,  O   make us  not  dependent  on  the  gifts  and  loans  of  men  but  rather  on  thy full,  open  and  generous  hand,  that  we  may  never  be  put  to  shame and  disgrace.
