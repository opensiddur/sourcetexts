Psalm  128
A   Pilgrim  Song.  Happy  is  everyone  who  reveres  the  Lord,  who walks  in  his  ways.  When  you  eat  of  the  toil  of  your  hands,  you shall  be  happy  and  at  ease.  Your  wife  shall  be  like  a   fruitful  vine in  the  interior  of  your  house,  your  children  like  olive  plants around  your  table.  Behold,  thus  indeed  shall  the  man  be  blessed who  reveres  the  Lord.  The  Lord  bless  you  from  Zion;  may  you see  the  welfare  of  Jerusal
