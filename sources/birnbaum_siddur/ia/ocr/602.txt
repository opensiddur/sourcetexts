When  the  Egyptians  were  drowning  in  the  Red  Sea  on  the seventh  day  of  Pesah,  God  restrained  the  angels  from  singing  his  praise,  say- ing: “How  can  you  sing  hymns  while  my  creatures  are  drowning  in  the  sea?” (Megillah  10b).
