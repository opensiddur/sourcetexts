11.  The  guilt-offering  which  a   cured  leper  had  to  bring  was  unlike  all other  guilt-offerings  in  this,  that  some  of  its  blood  was  sprinkled  on  the  person who  offered  it  (Leviticus  14:13-14).  On  account  of  this  peculiarity  none  of  the rules  connected  with  other  offerings  would  apply  to  that  brought  by  a   cured leper,  had  not  the  Torah  expressly  added:  “As  the  sin-offering  so  is  the  guilt- offering.”
