Psalm  120
A   Pilgrim  Song.  In  my  distress  I   called  to  the  Lord,  and  lie answered  me.  0   Lord,  save  me  from  lying  lips,  from  a   deceitful tongue.  What  will  a   deceitful  tongue  give  you,  what  will  it  profit you?  Sharp  arrows  of  a   warrior  with  burning  coals  of  broom!  Woe is  me  that  I   dwell  in  Meshek,  that  I   reside  amid  the  tents  of  Kedur. Too  long  have  I   been  living  where  men  hate  peace.  I   am  all  for peace;  but  when  I   speak,  they  are  for  war.
Psalm  121
A   Pilgrim  Song.  I   lift  my  eyes  to  the  hills;  whence  will  my  help come?  My  help  comes  from  the  Lord  who  made  heaven  and  earth. He  will  not  let  your  foot  slip;  he  who  guards  you  will  not  slumber.
