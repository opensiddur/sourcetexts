Why  should  I   be  afraid  in  days  of  evil,  when  the  iniquity  of my  foes  surrounds  me,  those  who  trust  in  their  wealth,  and  boast of  their  great  riches?  No  man  can  redeem  his  brother  [from  death], nor  give  to  God  a   ransom  for  him— for  too  costly  is  the  ransom  of one’s  soul  and  can  never  be — that  he  should  go  on  living  forever, that  he  should  not  see  the  grave.  Surely,  one  must  see  that  wise men  die,  that  the  stupid  and  senseless  perish  alike,  and  leave their  wealth  to  others.  Their  inward  consolation  is  that  their houses  shall  continue  forever,  and  their  homes  throughout  all generations;  they  name  estates  after  themselves.  But  man  abides not  in  his  splendor;  he  is  like  the  beasts  that  perish.
