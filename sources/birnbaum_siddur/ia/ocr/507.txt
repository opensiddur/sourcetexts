Psalm  130
A   Pilgrim  Song.  Out  of  the  depths  I   call  to  thee,  0   Lord.  O Lord,  hear  my  voice;  let  thy  ears  be  attentive  to  my  supplicating voice.  If  thou,  O   Lord,  shouldst  keep  strict  account  of  iniquities, O   Lord,  who  could  live  on?  But  with  thee  there  is  forgiveness, that  thou  mayest  be  revered.  I   look  for  the  Lord,  my  whole  being hopes;  I   wait  for  his  word.  My  soul  waits  for  the  Lord  more eagerly  than  watchmen  for  the  dawn,  than  watchmen  for  the  dawn. O   Israel,  put  your  hope  in  the  Lord,  for  with  the  Lord  there  is kindness;  with  him  there  Is  great  saving  power.  It  is  he  who  will redeem  Israel  from  all  its  iniquities.
Psalm  131
A   Pilgrim  Song  by  David.  O   Lord,  my  heart  is  not  haughty, nor  are  my  eyes  lofty;  neither  do  I   concern  myself  with  matters too  great  and  too  wonderful  for  me.  Surely  I   have  soothed  and stilled  my  soul,  like  a   weaned  child  with  its  mother;  my  soul  is with  me  like  a   weaned  child.  0   Israel,  put  your  hope  in  the  Lord, henceforth  and  forever.
Psalm  131  is  a   song  of  child-like  humility.  As  the  child  that  has  gone through  the  troublesome  process  of  weaning  can  lie  happily  in  its  mother’s arms,  so  the  psalmist’s  soul  has  found  contentment  and  happiness  through the  discipline  of  humility.
