Rabbi  Hananyah  ben  Teradyon  said:  If  two  sit  together and  no  words  of  Torah  are  spoken  between  them,  they  are  a   session of  scoffers,  of  whom  it  is  said:  “[A  good  man]  does  not  sit  in  the company  of  scoffers.”1  But  when  two  sit  together  and  interchange words  of  the  Torah,  the  Shekhinah  abides  between  them,  as  it  is said :   “Then  those  who  revered  the  Lord  spoke  to  each  other,  and the  Lord  listened  and  heard,  and  in  his  presence  a   record  was written  of  those  who  revere  the  Lord  and  respect  his  name.”2  Now, this  verse  refers  to  two  persons;  whence  do  we  know  that  even  if one  person  engages  in  the  study  of  the  Torah,  the  Holy  One, blessed  be  he,  determines  his  reward?
