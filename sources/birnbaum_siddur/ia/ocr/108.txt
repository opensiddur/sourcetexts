Originally,  the  Shemoneh Esreh  consisted  of  eighteen  blessings;  in  its  present  form,  however,  there  are nineteen.  The  addition  of  the  paragraph  concerning  the  slanderers  was  made toward  the  end  of  the  first  century  at  the  direction  of  Rabban  Gamaliel  II, head  of  the  Sanhedrin  of  Yabneh.  The  Talmud  offers  a   variety  of  reasons  for the  number  eighteen.  It  corresponds  to  the  eighteen  times  God  is  mentioned in  Psalm  29  as  well  as  in  the  Shema.  The  three  patriarchs,  Abraham,  Isaac and  Jacob  are  mentioned  eighteen  times  in  the  Bible.  This  number  also  cor- responds to  the  eighteen  vertebrae  of  the  spinal  column  (Berakhoth  28b).
