  Give  to  the  Lord  the  glory  due  to his  name;  worship  the  Lord  in  holy  array.  The  voice  of  the  Lord peals  across  the  waters;  it  is  the  God  of  glory  thundering!  The Lord  is  over  the  vast  waters.  The  voice  of  the  Lord  is  mighty;  the voice  of  the  Lord  is  majestic.  The  voice  of  the  Lord  breaks  the cedars;  the  Lord  shatters  the  cedars  of  Lebanon.  He  makes  Leb- anon and  Sirion  leap  like  a   calf,  like  a   wild  ox.  The  voice  of  the Lord  strikes  flames  of  fire;  the  voice  of  the  Lord  causes  the  desert to  tremble;  the  Lord  causes  the  desert  of  Kadesh  to  tremble.  The voice  of  the  Lord  whirls  the  oaks,  and  strips  the  woods  bare;  in  his palace  everything  says:  “Glory.”  The  Lord  sat  enthroned  at  the flood;  the  Lord  remains  King  forever.  The  Lord  will  give  strength to  his  people;  the  Lord  will  bless  his  people  with  peace.
