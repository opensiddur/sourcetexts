Torah  is  echoed  in  this  prayer,  in  which  the  merciful  Father  is  entreated  to enlighten  our  eyes  and  our  minds  to  understand  his  teachings.  This  is  the second  of  the  two  blessings  preceding  the  Shema,  “11a  "W  being  the  first.  As Psalm  19  praises  God  first  for  the  sun  and  then  for  the  Torah  which  en- lightens the  mind,  so  have  we  in  these  two  blessings  first  a   thanksgiving  for natural  light,  then  a   thanksgiving  for  spiritual  enlightenment.  As  in  the  case with  all  the  prayers,  occasional  variations  have  been  introduced  here  in  the course  of  many  centuries.
