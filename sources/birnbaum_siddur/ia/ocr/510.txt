The  custom  of reading  Pirke  Avoth  (“Chapters  of  the  Fathers”)  on  Sabbath  afternoons  was originally  limited,  it  seems,  to  the  period  between  Pesah  and  Shaimoth.  A sixth  chapter,  derived  from  a   source  other  than  the  Mishnah,  was  added  to the  five  chapters  of  Avoth  in  order  to  provide  a   separate  chapter  for  each  of the  six  Sabbaths  between  the  two  festivals.
