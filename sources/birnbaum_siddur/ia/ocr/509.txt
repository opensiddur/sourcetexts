Psalm  133
A   Pilgrim  Song  by  David.  Behold,  how  good  and  pleasant it  is  for  brethren  to  dwell  together  in  unity!  It  is  like  the  precious oil  upon  the  head,  flowing  down  the  beard,  Aaron’s  beard,  that comes  down  upon  the  edge  of  his  robes.  It  is  like  the  dew  of Mount  Hermon,  that  comes  down  upon  the  hills  of  Zion;  for  there the  Lord  commanded  the  blessing,  life  for  evermore.

A   Pilgrim  Song.  Come  bless  the  Lord,  all  you  servants  of  the Lord,  who  nightly  stand  in  the  house  of  the  Lord.  Lift  up  your hands  in  holiness,  and  bless  the  Lord.  May  the  Lord,  who  made heaven  and  earth,  bless  you  from  Zion.
