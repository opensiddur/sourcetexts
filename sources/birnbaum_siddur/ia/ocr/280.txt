Various  reasons  are given  for  the  recital  of  this  chapter  from  the  Mishnah,  which  deals  with  the oils  and  wicks  appropriate  for  the  Sabbath  lights.  Rashi  in  his  Siddur  (page 243)  says  that  this  chapter  is  recited  after  the  Sabbath  eve  service  so  as  to enable  the  late-comers  to  complete  their  prayers  and  leave  the  synagogue together  with  the  rest  of  the  congregation.  Accordingly,  this  chapter  is  omit- ted on  festival  occasions  when  late-coming  is  not  likely  to  happen.  Rabbi Isaiah  Horowitz  and  Rabbi  Jacob  Emden,  in  their  respective  editions  of  the Siddur,  are  of  the  opinion  that  Bammeh  Madlikin  is  to  be  recited  before Kabbalath  Shabbath.
