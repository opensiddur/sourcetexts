I   Chronicles  16:8-36
Give  thanks  to  the  Lord,  call  upon  his  name;  make  known  his deeds  among  the  peoples.  Sing  to  him,  sing  praises  to  him;  speak of  all  his  wonders.  Take  pride  in  his  holy  name;  let  the  heart  of those  who  seek  the  Lord  rejoice.  Inquire  of  the  Lord  and  his  might; seek  his  presence  continually.  Remember  the  wonders  he  has  done, his  marvels,  and  the  judgments  of  his  mouth,  O   descendants  of Israel  his  servant,  children  of  Jacob,  his  chosen.  He  is  the  Lord our  God;  his  judgments  are  over  all  the  earth.
