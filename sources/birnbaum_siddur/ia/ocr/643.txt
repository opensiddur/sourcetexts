In  memory  of  Jewish  martyrs:
May  God  remember  the  souls  of  the  saintly  martyrs  who  have been  slaughtered,  burned,  drowned  or  strangled  for  their  loyalty to  God.  We  pledge  charity  in  their  memory  and  pray  that  their souls  be  kept  among  the  immortal  souls  of  Abraham,  Isaac,  Jacob, Sarah,  Rebekah,  Rachel,  Leah,  and  all  the  righteous  men  and women  in  paradise;  and  let  us  say,  Amen.
