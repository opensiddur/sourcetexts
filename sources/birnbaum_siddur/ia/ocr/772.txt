BEFORE  A   JOURNEY
May  it  be  thy  will,  Lord  our  God  and  God  of  our  fathers,  to lead  us  on  safely  and  march  us  safely,  to  guide  us  safely  and  bring us  to  our  destination  in  life,  happiness  and  peace.  Deliver  us  from every  lurking  enemy  and  danger  on  the  road.  Let  us  obtain  favor, kindness  and  love  from  thee  and  from  all  who  see  us.  Hear  our supplication,  for  thou  art  God  who  hearest  prayer  and  suppli- cation. Blessed  art  thou,  O   Lord,  who  hearest  prayer.
