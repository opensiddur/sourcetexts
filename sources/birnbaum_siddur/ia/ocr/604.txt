Psalm  114
When  Israel  went  out  of  Egypt,  Jacob’s  household  from  a people  of  strange  speech,  Judah  became  God’s  sanctuary,  Israel  his dominion.  The  sea  beheld  and  fled;  the  Jordan  turned  backward; the  mountains  skipped  like  rams,  and  the  hills  like  lambs.  What ails  you,  O   sea,  that  thus  you  flee?  Why,  O   Jordan,  do  you  turn backward?  You  mountains,  why  do  you  skip  like  rams?  You  hills, why  do  you  leap  like  lambs?  Tremble,  O   earth,  at  the  Lord’s  pres- ence, at  the  presence  of  the  God  of  Jacob,  who  turns  the  rock  into a   pool  of  water,  the  flint  into  a   flowing  fountain.
