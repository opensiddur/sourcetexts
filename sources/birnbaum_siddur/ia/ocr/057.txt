The  incense  was  composed  of  the  following  eleven  kinds  of spices:  balm,  onycha,  galbanum,  and  frankincense,  seventy  minas’ Weight  of  each;  myrrh,  cassia,  spikenard,  and  saffron,  sixteen minas’  weight  of  each;  twelve  minas  of  costus;  three  minas  of  an aromatic  bark;  and  nine  minas  of  cinnamon.  [Added  to  the  spices were]  nine  kabs  of  Karsina  lye,  three  seahs  and  three  kabs  of Cyprus  wine — if  Cyprus  wine  could  not  be  obtained,  strong  white wine  might  be  substituted  for  it — a   fourth  of  a   kab  of  Sodom  salt, and  a   minute  quantity  of  ma'aleh  ashan  [a  smoke-producing  in- gredient]. Rabbi  Nathan  says:  A   minute  quantity  of  Jordan  amber was  also  required.
