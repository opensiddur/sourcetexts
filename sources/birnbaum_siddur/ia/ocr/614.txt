Musaf  for  Rosh  Hodesfi
Those  opposite  them  say:  Blessed —
Blessed  be  the  glory  of  the  Lord  from  his  abode.1
And  in  thy  holy  Scriptures  it  is  written:
The  Lord  shall  reign  forever,
Your  God,  O   Zion,  for  all  generations.
Praise  the  Lord.2
Reader:
Through  all  generations  we  will  declare  thy  greatness;  to  all eternity  we  will  proclaim  thy  holiness;  thy  praise,  our  God,  shall never  depart  from  our  mouth,  for  thou  art  a   great  and  holy  God and  King.  Blessed  art  thou,  0   Lord,  holy  God.
Thou  art  holy  and  thy  name  is  holy,  and  holy  beings  praise thee  daily.  Blessed  art  thou,  O   Lord,  holy  God.
