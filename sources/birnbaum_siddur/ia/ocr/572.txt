Though  a   thousand  fall  at  your  side,  and  a myriad  at  your  right  hand,  it  shall  not  come  near  you.  Only  with your  eyes  will  you  gaze,  and  see  the  reward  of  evil  men.  Thou,  O Lord,  art  my  refuge!  When  you  have  made  the  Most  High  your shelter,  no  disaster  shall  befall  you,  no  calamity  shall  come  near your  home.  For  he  will  give  his  angels  charge  over  you,  to  guard you  in  all  your  ways.  They  will  bear  you  in  their  hands,  lest  you strike  your  foot  against  a   stone.  You  can  tread  on  lion  and  asp; you  can  trample  young  lion  and  serpent.  “He  clings  to  me,  so  I   de- liver him ;   I   set  him  safe,  because  he  loves  my  name.  When  he  calls upon  me,  I   will  answer  him;  I   will  be  with  him  in  trouble;  I   will rescue  him  and  bring  him  to  honor.  With  long  life  will  I   satisfy  him, and  let  him  see  my  saving  power.”
