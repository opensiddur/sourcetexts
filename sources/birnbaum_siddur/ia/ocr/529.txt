Rabbi  Dostai  ben  Yannai  said  in  the  name  of  Rabbi Meir:  Whoever  forgets  anything  of  what  he  has  learned,  Scripture regards  him  as  if  he  committed  a   grave  sin,  for  it  is  said:  “Only  take care,  and  watch  yourself  well  that  you  do  not  forget  the  things which  jour  eyes  saw.”.
