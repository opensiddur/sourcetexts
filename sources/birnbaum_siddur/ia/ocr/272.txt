Psalm  29
A   Psalm  of  David.  Give  to  the  Lord,  0   heavenly  beings,  give  to the  Lord  honor  and  glory.
Psalm  29  describes  the  manifestation  of  God’s  power  in  the  thunderstorm
and  the  flood,  and  ends  with  an  assurance  of  his  favor  to  his  people.
