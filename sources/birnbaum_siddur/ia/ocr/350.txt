Morning  Service  for  Sabbaths  and  Festivals
May  the  glory  of  the  Lord  be  forever;  may  the  Lord  rejoice  in his  works.  Blessed  be  the  name  of  the  Lord  henceforth  and  for- ever. From  the  rising  of  the  sun  to  its  setting  let  the  Lord’s  name be  praised.  High  above  all  nations  is  the  Lord;  above  the  heavens is  his  glory.  0   Lord,  thy  name  is  forever;  0   Lord,  thy  fame  is through  all  generations.  The  Lord  has  set  up  his  throne  in  the heavens,  and  his  kingdom  rules  over  all.  Let  the  heavens  rejoice, let  the  earth  exult,  and  let  them  say  among  the  nations,  “The Lord  is  King!”  The  Lord  is  King,  the  Lord  was  King,  the  Lord shall  be  King  forever  and  ever.  The  Lord  is  King  for  evermore; the  heathen  have  vanished  from  his  land.  The  Lord  annuls  the counsel  of  nations;  he  foils  the  plans  of  peoples.
