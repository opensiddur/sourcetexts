Our  glory  has  waned  among  the  nations;  they  utterly  detest  us. How  long  shall  thy  glory  remain  in  captivity,  and  thy  splendor  in the  hand  of  the  foe?  Arouse  thy  might  and  thy  zeal  against  thy enemies,  that  they  may  be  put  to  shame  and  crushed  despite  their power;  let  not  our  sufferings  seem  trivial  to  thee.  May  thy  com- passion hasten  to  our  aid  in  the  day  of  our  trouble;  if  not  for  our sake,  act  for  thy  own  sake,  and  destroy  not  our  mere  remnant. Be  gracious  to  a   people,  who  fervently  proclaim  thy  Oneness  twice a   day,  saying:  “Hear,  O   Israel,  the  Lord  is  our  God,  the  Lord  is One.”1
