The fragrant  smoke  of  incense  rising  towards  heaven  was  a   natural  symbol  of prayer  ascending  to  God.  From  Psalm  141:2  (“Lot  my  prayer  rise  like  irieoni  i before  thee”)  it  appears  that  the  incense-offering  symbolized  prayer.
