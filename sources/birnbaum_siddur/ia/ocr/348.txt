Morning  Service  for  Sabbaths  and  Festivals
The  eye  of  the  Lord  rests  on  those  who  revere  him,  those  who hope  for  his  kindness,  to  save  them  from  death  and  to  keep  them alive  in  famine.  Our  soul  waits  for  the  Lord;  he  is  our  help  and our  shield.  In  him  our  heart  rejoices;  in  his  holy  name  we  trust. May  thy  kindness,  0   Lord,  rest  on  us,  even  as  our  hope  rests in  thee.
