He  did  great, incomprehensible  acts  and  countless  wonders;  he  kept  us  alive, and  did  not  let  us  slip.1  He  made  us  tread  upon  the  high  places of  our  enemies,  and  raised  our  strength  over  all  our  foes.  He performed  for  us  miracles  and  vengeance  upon  Pharaoh,  signs and  wonders  in  the  land  of  the  Hamites;  he  smote  in  his  wrath all  the  first-born  of  Egypt,  and  brought  his  people  Israel  from their  midst  to  enduring  freedom.  He  made  his  children  pass  be- tween the  divided  parts  of  the  Red  Sea,  and  engulfed  their  pursuers and  their  enemies  in  the  depths.  His  children  beheld  his  might; they  gave  praise  and  thanks  to  his  name,  and  willingly  accepted his  sovereignty.
Moses  and  the  children  of  Israel  sang  a   song  to  thee  with  great rejoicing;  all  of  them  said:
“Who  is  like  thee,  0   Lord,  among  the  mighty?  Who  is  like  thee, glorious  in  holiness,  awe-inspiring  in  renown,  doing  wonders?”2
Thy  children  saw  thy  majesty  as  thou  didst  part  the  sea  before Moses.  “This  is  my  God!’.’  they  shouted,  and  they  said:
“The  Lord  shall  reign  forever  and  ever.”3
