Burial  Service
May  his  great  name  be  blessed  forever  and  to  all  eternity.
Blessed  and  praised,  glorified  and  exalted,  extolled  and  honored, adored  and  lauded  be  the  name  of  the  Holy  One,  blessed  be  he, beyond  all  the  blessings  and  hymns,  praises  and  consolations  that are  ever  spoken  in  the  world;  and  say,  Amen.
May  there  be  abundant  peace  from  heaven,  and  life,  for  us  and for  all  Israel;  and  say,  Amen.
He  who  creates  peace  in  his  celestial  heights,  may  he  create peace  for  us  and  for  all  Israel;  and  say,  Amen.
On  leaving  the  burial  ground  all  wash  their  hands  and  say:
He  will  destroy  death  forever;  the  Lord  God  will  wipe  away tears  from  every  face,  and  will  remove  from  all  the  earth  all  insult against  his  people;  for  the  Lord  has  spoken.1
