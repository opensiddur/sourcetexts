By  means  of  this  eruv  may  we  be  permitted  to  bake,  cook, keep  dishes  warm,  light  Sabbath  lights,  and  prepare  during  the festival  all  we  need  for  the  Sabbath — we  and  all  Israelites  that live  in  this  town.
LIGHTING  THE  FESTIVAL  LIGHTS
Blessed  art  thou,  Lord  our  God,  King  of  the  universe,  who hast  sanctified  us  with  thy  commandments,  and  commanded  us to  light  (the  Sabbath  and)  the  festival  lights.
On  the  first  night  add:
Blessed  art  thou,  Lord  our  God,  King  of  the  universe,  who hast  granted  us  life  and  sustenance  and  permitted  us  to  reach this  season.
AMIDAH  FOR  FESTIVALS
The  Amidah  is  recited  in  silent  devotion  while  standing,  facing  east.
(When  I   proclaim  the  name  of  the  Lord,  give  glory  to  our  God !)
0   Lord,  open  thou  my  lips,  that  my  mouth  may  declare  thy praise.
