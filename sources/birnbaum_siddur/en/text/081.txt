{{c|''Psalm'' 100}}

A psalm for the thank-offering.<ref>{{He|מזמור לתודה|a psalm for the thank-offering}} was recited in the Temple on weekdays when thank-offerings were presented. The psalmist invites the whole world to join Israel in the worship of God and to acknowledge him as the merciful Father of all mankind.</ref>

Shout praise to the Lord, all the earth. Serve the Lord with joy; come before him with singing. Know that the Lord is God; he has made us, and we are his, his people and the flock of his pasture. Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise; give thanks to him, bless his name. For the Lord is good; his kindness is forever; his faithfulness endures to all generations. 

May the glory of<ref>{{He|יהי כבוד...|may the glory of...}} that is, may the glory of the Lord, the universe, remain forever; may God always be pleased with his creation and preserve it.</ref> the Lord be forever; may the Lord rejoice in his works.
