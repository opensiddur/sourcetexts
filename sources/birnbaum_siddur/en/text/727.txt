He was kept at a distance from a people as turbulent as water.

feature was the procession which accompanied the priest who had been allotted the duty of drawing the water for the libation ceremony from the pool of Siloam at Jerusalem. According to tradition, "whoever has not witnessed this celebration has never seen real rejoicing" (Sukkah 5:1). It was called {{Hebrew missing}} ("the joyous procession to and from the well") because from there they drew inspiration. Compare Isaiah 12:3 ("With joy shall you draw water from the fountains of deliverance’’).

This benediction is called {{Hebrew missing}} since it recounts the omnipotence of God.
