Thou who art holy and awe-inspiring, save us; thou who art merciful and gracious, prosper us; thou who keepest the covenant, answer us when we eall.

Thou who supportest men of integrity, save us; thou who art mighty forever, prosper us; thou who art blameless in thy deeds, answer us when we call.
{{Dhr}}
:Thrill with joy over the Torah!
:Render glory to the Torah!
:She is more precious than jewels.
:We exult over this Torah,
:For she is our strength and our light.

:I sing joyous praise to my God,
:And I place all my hope in him.
:I praise him amidst his people,
:My Creator, in whom I trust.

:With all my heart I sing thy faith,
:Ever rendering thy praises.
:As long as I live I will tell
:Of thy wonders and thy kindness.
:Let us exult in this Torah,
:For she is our strength and our light.
