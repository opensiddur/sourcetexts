{{c|''I Chronicles'' 29:10-13}}

David blessed the Lord before all the assembly, and David said: Blessed art thou, O Lord, God of Israel our father, forever and ever. Thine, O Lord, is the greatness and the power, the glory
and the victory and the majesty, for all that is in heaven and on earth is thine; thine, O Lord, is the kingdom, and thou art supreme over all. Riches and honor come from thee; thou rulest over all; in thy hand are power and might, and it is in thy power to make all great and strong. Hence, our God, we ever thank thee and praise thy glorious name.
