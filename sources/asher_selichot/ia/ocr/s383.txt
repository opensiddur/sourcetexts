Ps. xx.—To Chief Musician, a Psalm of David.
The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the
God of Jacob defend thee. Send thee help from the sanctuary,
and strengthen thee out of Zion. Remember all thy offerings,
and accept thy burnt sacrifice. Selah. Grant thee according
to thine own heart, and fulfil all thy counsel. We will rejoice
in thy salvation, and in the name of our God we will set up our
banners ; the Lord fulfil all thy petitions. Now know I that
the Lord saveth his anointed ; he will hear him from his holy
heaven with the saving strength of his right hand. Some ¢rust
in chariots, and some in horses : but we will remember the name
of the Lord our God. They are brought down and fallen: but
we are risen and stand upright. Save, Lord; let the king hear
us when we call.
