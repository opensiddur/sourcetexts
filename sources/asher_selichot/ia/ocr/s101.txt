O Lord God of Hosts, adored by higher beings, thou saidst,
Return, ye refractory children, come ye before me with praise and
thanksgiving, seek ye my countenance with tears and fervent
supplications : though mere lip service remains, unanswered, yet
to the truly penitent the gates of mercy are open. Thy word
