PROPITIATORY PRAYERS FOR THE
FIRST DAY.
The Propitiatory Prayers are commenced on the Sunday before
New Year, except when the Festival occurs on Monday or Tuesday, when
they are begun instead on the Sunday week previous to the Festival.
These Prayers are said at an early hour before the Morning Service.
