Our God, and the God of our fathers.
Thou art my portion, and the rock of my heart. I have de-
aired thee in the night on my couch, unto thee I call when my
heart is overwhelmed. We have been treacherous, and have
repeatedly excited thine anger ; we have been trodden down
and crushed, because no good deeds pleaded for us. Our distress
hath greatly increased, for we have erred in vision, we have
stumbled in judgment, and all our righteousness is as filthy rags.
