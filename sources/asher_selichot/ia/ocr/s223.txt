It is Israel’s hope that God’s loving-kindness ceaseth not, he
waiteth for his everlasting unfailing faithfulness, yet the eyes of
those who hope for redemption are running; those who pine
in the valley of tears cast down their moist eyes. What had
flourished and grown high is now deeply bowed down; the
head hath become the tail, and the essential unimportant ;
those who sat in darkness and obscurity, waiting for the end,
hope for light, but clouds and darkness still surround them.
