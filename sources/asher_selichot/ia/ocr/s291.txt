let thy righteousness, in which is our trust, be verified ; be mind-
ful of the virtues of our forefathers, dismiss us not empty from
thy presence, and forgive our sins and iniquities. Thy rod and
thy staff, O thou who dwellest on high, are the consolation of the
least numerous of nations. Let our supplication ascend to thee
heavenwards, O God and King, who sittest on the throne of
mercy !
