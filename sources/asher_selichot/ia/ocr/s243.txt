as the apple of his eye ; to that All-powerful one, whose praise
is in every one’s mouth—alas! the memory of him will not
suffer me to sleep. His arm redeemed me from those unchaste
people (the Egyptians), he adorned me with new garments
(the Torah), spoke with me by the mouth of his saints (the pro-
