his master may justly claim his wages; but he who neglecteth
his work hath to thank his master’s generosity if he rewardeth
him ; equally so there is mercy and forgiveness with the Lord
our God, though we have rebelled against him. Such are his
sublime and worthy attributes ; he setteth a bright example,
teaching us to feed even the refractory. The remnant of Joseph
hope to find forgiveness for their iniquities ; O reward them as
Joseph did to his brethren, good for evil, and we will for ever
extol thy righteousness.
