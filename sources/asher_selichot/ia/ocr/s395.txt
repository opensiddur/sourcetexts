of us. Check thy punishing hand in judgment; though con-
victed guilt come before thee, do not blot our name from thy
book! When thou comest to search our conduct, let thy tender
mercies anticipate thy wrath; when thou seest the poverty of
our deeds, assist us with thy righteousness; teach us when we
ery unto thee ; command our salvation when we entreat thee,
and bring again the captivity of Jacob’s tents. Behold, their
gates are desolate; remember thy promise that the law shall not
be forgotten out of the mouths of their seed! Break the seal of
the testimony, and reveal thy secret tv those who study thee,
so that they who diffuse light may never lack the seasoning of
truth. O Lord, acknowledge those who acknowledge thee, have
mercy on those who call upon thee, and bring back the prisoners
