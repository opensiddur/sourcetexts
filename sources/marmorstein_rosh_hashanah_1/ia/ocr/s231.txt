brought the words of God from Sinai, who led the firstborn

Lord ten times he shared the penalty, his death was announced
to him ten times; and when he reached the border of the Holy
Land he was restrained. Judges, when they judge through
bribery, how can they expect to be judged with charity?
As they have judged others, so shall it be done to them.
The youths (Nadab and Abibu) who offered up strange fire,
suffered the decree of wrath as a warning to evil doers among
the dispersed people (Israel). The fool desired what was
banned, he too was caught within the ban with all that was
his, the heaps of corn, the standing corn and vineyard. What
avail his deeds to the faithless and the robber, substance avails
not on the day of wrath, only entreaty before Him whose
teaching avails. He (Usha) stretched out his hand to grasp
the Holy Ark, and judgment and wrath overtook him; what
shall they do who pass before Him like lambs? When the
skilled player on the harp (David) said, ‘Test me’, he was
tested; but then he said, ‘Since I am without merit, be lenient
with me, do not come in judgment to judge me’. They who
call out aloud and blow the Shofar, when their misdeeds are
counted in judgment, as an undeserved gift let them be atoned
this day. The prince (Uzziah), when he dared to bring incense
to the Lord, was marked on the forehead with distinct
leprosy and so was he judged for unwitting transgression.
When there falters the foot of the proud, who arrogantly
entered the Sanctuary, they too will come to be judged by
Him who humbles the proud. When those who act falsely
come to be tested, with angry argument and question to be
judged, then the mouth of those who speak falsehood, will
be silenced. The upright (Hezekiah) walked before Him in
truth, yet after his good deeds and his truth, the God of truth
condemned one of his deeds. The favourite (Josiah) whom
none among the chosen of the Lord resembled before or after,
was yet severely judged by the Lord. So let all creatures on
