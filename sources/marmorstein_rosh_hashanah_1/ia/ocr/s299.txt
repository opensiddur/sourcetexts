the whole year is banished by the sound of the Shofar. The
form of the nine benedictions corresponds to the nine Shofar
blasts; in His purity He allows them to entreat through the
prescribed sound of the Shofar. Even those stained with evil,
when they repent of transgression, Thou dost soothe their
wound at the sound of the cry of the Shofar. Better than the
sacrificial bullock and the splendid ram is the day appointed
for the sound of renewal of the Shofar. The evil impulse is
broken and the accuser disgraced and the upright heart
refreshed at the sound of beauty of the Shofar. When once
they blew the Shofar (before Jericho), they cleaved asunder
the valley; they caused cities to sink through the tumult of
the sound of the Shofar. All the multitude of the wicked will
shake with trembling when they cry out aloud with a sound
as piercing as the Shofar. When His voice is heard, every
settlement is terrified, He blows away peoples in a hurricane

with its voice. To the God of my salvation from the teeth
of iron I cried and from before the trampling foot I shrieked
with my voice. He has not made known to me the measure
of my end, when in my land the turtle dove will make heard
its voice. Even from scholars has He concealed my end, that
my seers may not know the time when they will lift up their
voice. Pray look and see my woe and my pain, there is no
friend to me to whom I can raise my voice. For ever shall
I hope that no word shall be omitted from those who listen
for a still small voice. In my midst my heart rejoices when
I hear my Beloved sounding on my doors with His voice.
