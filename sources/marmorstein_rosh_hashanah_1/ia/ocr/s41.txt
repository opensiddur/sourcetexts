2 Prayers 2
On entering the Synagogue:
And I through the abundance of Thy grace enter Thy house
and bow down before Thy holy place in awe of Thee.
In the house of God let us walk in throngs.

O Israel. And I through the abundance of Thy grace enter Thy‏
house and bow down before Thy holy place in awe of Thee.‏
O Lord, I love the site of Thy house, the place of the dwelling‏
of Thy glory. I bow, throw myself down and kneel before the‏
Lord, my Maker. And as for me, my prayer is to Thee, O‏
Lord, at a time of favour. O God, in the abundance of Thy‏

In the morning I seek Thee, my rock and my fortress.‏ שחר

me. Behold, man’s song pleases Thee. Therefore, I wish to‏
thank Thee as long as God’s breath is in me.‏
