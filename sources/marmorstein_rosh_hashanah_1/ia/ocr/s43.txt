The Thirteen Principles
Exalted is the living God and praised. He exists and‏ ינדל
timeless is His existence.
He is unique and there is nought unique like His
uniqueness. He is hidden and infinite too is His unity.
He has not the shape of a body nor is He a body. We
cannot estimate His sanctity.
He was there before anything that was created; He is
the first and there was no beginning to His beginning.
Behold He is the Master of the world and every crea-
ture indicates His grandeur and His rule.
The wealth of His prophecy did He bestow upon men
of His possession and His glory.
No other prophet arose in Israel like Moses, who
perceived His likeness.
The true Torah has God given to His people through
His prophet, the faithful one of His house.
God will not alter nor will He change His law for
another unto eternity.
He observes and knows our secrets, perceiving even
at its beginning the end of a matter.
He treats man kindly according to his conduct and
sends disaster to the wicked according to his wickedness.
He will send our Messiah at the end of days to redeem
those who wait for the finality of His salvation.
God will revive the dead in the abundance of His
grace. Blessed for ever and ever be His renowned Name.
