yos Those who resolutely subdue their desire, they too
were found guilty when examined in justice as though no
delight had been found in them. The first man formed from
the dust of the earth, who was comparable to the heavenly
angels, failed to understand and he became like a mere
shadow. To till and care for the garden had God set him
apart; when he transgressed His commandment, then he
drove him away; but this day became his intercessor. When
He awoke that radiant one (Abraham) from the dark, the
world had been empty and dark, after twenty generations
despised and dejected; though he showed the straight and
even path to those who had strayed, yet since he himself
said ‘how shall I know’, it was decreed against him ‘you shall
surely know’ (the sufferings of your descendants). As for
those who pass through the valley of tears to be tested, when
the Lord judges the case according to their deeds, who will
then be justified before the Judge? The pure one who offered
his life as a sacrifice (Isaac), did not find comfort in his old
age, for his eyes became dim through the idolatrous smoke of
the fool (Esau). He who trained his children in the tents of
the Torah (Jacob), since he said ‘My way is hidden’, his
offspring (Joseph) was long concealed from him. The wicked
man who plots in secret, drives away justice and rejects it,
can he hide himself on the day of wrath? When the Lord
tested the twelve brothers, He did not pronounce them just,
for they had sold the righteous one for silver. The sons of
the nameless (Chaldeans), who sold Israel for lechery, wine
and violent plunder, how heavy will be their burden when
