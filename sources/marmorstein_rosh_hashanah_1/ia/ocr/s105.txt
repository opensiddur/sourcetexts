38 Morning Prayer for the New Year 38
Lord of our might, Rock of our fortress, shield of our salva-
tion, a fortress around us. Blessed God, great in knowledge,
He has prepared and made the sun’s rays, the Benevolent has
fashioned glory for His Name, lights has He placed around
His might, the chiefs of His hosts are holy, exalting the
Almighty, constantly do they recount the honour of God and
His holiness. Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, for the glory
of Thy handiwork, and for the radiant lights that Thou hast
made, they shall glorify Thee, Selah.

before His throne, grace and compassion before His glory.
Good are the lights which our God has created, He has
formed them with knowledge, reason and understanding.
Power and strength has He placed in them that they may
reign in the midst of the world. They are full of lustre and
brightly shine, comely is their lustre over the whole world.
They rejoice when they go forth and are jubilant when they
come in and fulfil in awe the will of their Owner. Glory and
honour do they give to His Name, joy and delight at the
mention of His Kingdom. He called to the sun, then light
shone forth, He saw and decreed the shape of the moon.
Praise does the whole host of heaven bestow on Him, fame
and grandeur the Seraphim, Ophanim and holy Hayoth.
