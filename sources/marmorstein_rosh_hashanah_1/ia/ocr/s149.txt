day of judgment. Who can endure it to justify himself in‏

and all on whom judgment is to be done, fear lest they will‏
not be favoured in judgment for there is no compassion in‏
judgment. He does not show respect for persons in judgment.‏
He is both witness and plaintiff in judgment, that there may‏
be known on earth His work in judgment. The hand of every‏
man seals the judgment, according to His rule does He‏
perform for him judgment, and according to man’s deeds‏
does He carry out judgment, man’s actions rebuke him in‏
judgment, a stone from the wall cries out in judgment, a‏
wooden beam answers in judgment, everything in truth and‏
everything in judgment with the attribute of compassion and‏
the attribute of judgment. This day is appointed for peoples in‏
judgment, it is assigned as a day of judgment, and on it all‏
souls will He bring to judgment. It has been made a statute‏
in memory of the first day and last day, to remember on it‏
the sleepers of Hebron, that in their shadow the lily of Sharon‏
may shelter, those who return to divine protection may be‏
acquitted on it, those who blow the Shofar and cry out aloud‏
in order to profit from the righteousness of their forefathers‏
and be saved through them from anger and wrath. Just as‏
the father (Abraham) kept to the path of justice and clemency,‏
so let kindness and clemency be done in judgment to his‏
descendants. Just as the patient dove (Isaac) sowed clemency,‏
so may his descendants reap truth and clemency. Just as‏
clemency was granted to the one flawless in truth (Jacob) so‏
may there be granted to his descendants truth and clemency.‏
The throne of glory is founded on clemency. In front of Him‏
stands a defender who pleads for clemency, and when the‏
Shofar sounds on behalf of those in need of clemency, then‏
the fathers of clemency entreat and implore and say before‏
Him who loves clemency: ‘“‘Far be it from Thee who dost‏
speak with clemency, to do justice without clemency, even‏
though in the place of justice there is no clemency and only‏
where there is no justice, is there clemency. But Thou in‏
judgment dost act with clemency to judge the world through‏
acts of clemency, for the foundation of Thy throne is judg-‏
ment and clemency; and when Thou dost examine deeds‏
lacking in clemency, remember with favour the few deeds‏
done with clemency, and with an unearned gift act with‏
clemency to open for them the treasuries of clemency.’‏
