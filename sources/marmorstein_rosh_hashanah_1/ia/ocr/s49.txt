Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,

who made for me all that I need.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who makes man’s steps firm.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who girds Israel with might.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who crowns Israel with splendour.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who gives strength to the weary.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who removes sleep from my eyes and slumber from my
eyelids.
