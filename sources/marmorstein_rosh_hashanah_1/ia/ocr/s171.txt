skin came to an end she put the child under one of the bushes.
Then she went away and sat down at a distance of a bow shot
because she thought: ‘Let me not see the child die’. She sat
down at a distance and raised her voice and wept. And God
heard the lad’s voice and an angel of God called to Hagar from
heaven and said to her: ‘What troubles you, Hagar? Do not be
afraid. God has heard the lad’s voice from where he is. Rise,
lift up the lad and hold him firmly with the hand, for I will
make him a great nation’. And God opened her eyes and she
saw a well of water and she went and filled the skin with water
and gave the lad to drink. And God was with the lad and
he grew up and lived in the wilderness and became an archer.
