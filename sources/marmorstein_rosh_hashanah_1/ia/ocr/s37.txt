22 Kiddush for the New Year 22
After the evening prayer but before yy9y the Kiddush is recited
over a glass of wine. At home too, it is recited before the meal, but on
the Sabbath preceded by ויבלן‎
(On Sabbath) ויהי‎ It was evening and it was morning, the
sixth day. The heavens and the earth were completed and all
their hosts. God completed by the seventh day His work that
He had done and on the seventh day He ceased from all his
work which He had done. And God blessed the seventh day
and sanctified it, for on it did He cease from all His work
which God as Creator had done.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has created the fruit of the vine.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has chosen us from all peoples and exalted us above all‏
tongues and sanctified us through His commandments. Thou‏
hast given us, O Lord our God, in love this day (of the‏
Sabbath and this Day) of Remembrance a day (of remem-‏
brance) of Teruah (in love) for a sacred convocation, a‏
reminder of the exodus from Egypt. For Thou hast chosen‏
us and sanctified us from all peoples and Thy word is truth‏
and endures for ever. Blessed art Thou, O Lord, King over‏
all the earth who sanctifies (the Sabbath and) Israel and the‏
Day of Remembrance.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has given us life, sustained us and allowed us to reach‏
this time.‏
After Kiddush at the meal, one recites הקמוציא‎ over the
two loaves. After tasting the bread one dips a piece of
sweet apple in honey and says:
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has created the fruit of the tree.‏
Then one eats the piece of apple and says:
May it find favour before Thee O Lord our God and‏ יהי
God of our fathers, to renew unto us a good and sweet year.‏
On the eve of the New Year friends and acquaintances express their
good wishes to one another in the following phrase:
May you be inscribed for a good year!
