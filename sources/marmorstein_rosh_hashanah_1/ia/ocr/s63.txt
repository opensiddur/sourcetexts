He established it as Law for Jacob, for Israel as an eteznal
convenant, saying: To you will I give the land of Canaan
as the portion of your inheritance, though you are small in
number, few and strangers in it. They wandered from nation
to nation, from one kingdom to another people. He let no
man oppress them and rebuked kings because of them. Do not
touch my anointed ones, to my prophets do no harm. Sing
to the Lord, all the earth, proclaim from day to day His
salvation. Tell of His glory among the nations and among all
peoples of His wonders. For great is the Lord and most
renowned, and more awe-inspiring than all deities. For all
the deities of the peoples are idols — the Lord has made the
heavens. Splendour and beauty are before Him, might and
glory are in His place. Ascribe to the Lord, O families of the
peoples, ascribe to the Lord honour and might. Ascribe to
the Lord the glory of His Name, bring a gift and come before
Him, bow before the Lord in sacred adornment. Tremble
before Him, all the earth, though the world be firm and does
not totter. Let the heavens rejoice and the earth revel and
among the nations let them say: The Lord reigns. Let the sea
roar and the fullness of it, let the field and all that is in it be
joyful. Then will trees of the forest exult before the Lord
when He comes to judge the earth. Thank the Lord for He
is good, for His grace endures for ever. And say: Save us,
God of our salvation, and gather us and deliver us from the
nations, so that we may thank Thy holy Name and extol Thy
fame. Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, from eternity
to eternity. And the whole people said Amen and praised the
Lord. Extol the Lord our God and bow before His footstool,
for He is holy. Extol the Lord our God and bow before His
holy mountain, for holy is the Lord our God. He is compas-
sionate, grants atonement for guilt and does not destroy;
many a time does He avert His anger nor does He arouse the
whole of His wrath. Thou, O Lord, dost not withold Thy
compassion from me, Thy grace and Thy truth always protect
me. Remember Thy compassion, O Lord, and Thine acts of
kindness for they are from eternity. Ascribe might to God,
