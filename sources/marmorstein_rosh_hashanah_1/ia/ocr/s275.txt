127 Musaph for the New Year 127
8/18 Our God and God of our fathers, reign over the whole
world in Thy glory and rise above the whole earth in Thy
grandeur. Shine forth in the exalted beauty of Thy might
upon all the inhabitants of the territory of Thine earth so
that every creature may recognize that Thou hast created it
and that every form may understand that Thou hast formed
it and all with breath in the nostrils may say: The Lord, the
God of Israel, is King and His Kingdom rules over all. (Our
God and God of our fathers, take pleasure in our rest.)
Sanctify us through Thy commandments and grant our portion
in Thy Torah, satisfy us with Thy goodness and gladden us
with Thy help (and grant us, O Lord our God, in love and
favour, Thy holy Sabbath as a portion so that Israel, who
sanctifies Thy Name, may rest on it) and purify our heart to
serve Thee in truth for Thou, O Lord, art truth and Thy word
is truth and endures for ever. Blessed art Thou, O Lord King
over the whole earth, who sanctifies (the Sabbath and) Israel
and the Day of Remembrance.
