Gracious and compassionate is the Lord, long suffering and

They speak of the glory of Thy dominion and tell of Thy

that are bent. All eyes hope for Thee and Thou givest them
their food in its time. Thou openest Thy hands and satisfiest
all the living in favour. Righteous is the Lord in all His ways
and pious in all His deeds. The Lord is near all who call Him,
all who call Him in truth. The desire of those that fear Him
does He do and He hears their cry and saves them. The Lord
protects all that love Him and destroys all the wicked. The
fame of the Lord does my mouth declare and all flesh shall
bless His holy Name for ever and ever. We too shall bless
the Lord from now to eternity. Halleluyah.
