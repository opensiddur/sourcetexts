On the Sabbath:
(Psalm 29) Psalm of David:
Ascribe to the Lord, sons of the
powerful, ascribe to the Lord
honour and might. Ascribe to the
Lord the honour of His Name,
bow down before the Lord in
holy array. The voice of the Lord
is above the waters, the God of
glory has thundered, the Lord is
above mighty waters. The voice
of the Lord is power, the voice
of the Lord is beauty. The voice
of the Lord shatters cedars, the
Lord shattered cedars of Lebanon.

unicorns. The voice of the Lord
makes the wilderness tremble,
the Lord makes the wilderness of

Lord causes hinds to calve, and
strips forests and in His sanctu-
ary everything expresses honour.
The Lord was enthroned as King
for eternity. The Lord gives
strength to His people, the Lord
blesses His people with peace.
