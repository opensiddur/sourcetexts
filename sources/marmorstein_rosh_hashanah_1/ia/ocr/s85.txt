O water above the heavens. Let them praise the Name of the
Lord for He commanded and they were created. He estab-
lished them for ever, for eternity, He gave a decree that will
not pass. Praise the Lord from the earth, dragons and all the
depths. Fire and hail, snow and smoke, the whirlwind obeys
His order, the mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all
cedars, the wild beast and all cattle, reptiles and winged
birds, earthly kings and all nations, princes and all earthly
judges, youths as well as maidens, old men with young lads.
Let them praise the Name of the Lord for His Name alone
is exalted. His majesty is above earth and heaven. He has
raised the condition of His people, there is fame for all His
pious ones, for the children of Israel, the people near Him,
Halleluyah.
(Psalm 149.) Halleluyah. Sing unto the Lord a new song,
His fame is in the gathering of the pious. Israel rejoices in
its Maker, the sons of Zion exult in their King. Let them
praise His Name with dancing, with timbrel and harp let them
play to Him. For the Lord favours His people, He adorns the
humble with victory. The pious revel in honour, they exult
upon their couches. Exaltations of God are in their throat
and a two-edged sword in their hand, so as to carry out
vengeance among the nations, chastisements among the pco-
ples, to bind their kings in fetters and their notables in chains
of iron, to execute on them the judgment that is written. He
is an adornment to all His pious ones. Halleluyah.
