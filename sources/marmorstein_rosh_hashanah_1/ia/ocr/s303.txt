thunder at the Revelation when sounds were given on the

a symbol of the threefold call of the Shofar (at the redemp-
tion) that will one day resound from the future Shofar. I pray
to heaven and my eyes are lifted up that I may be allowed to
see the gathering of the exiles at the loud sound of the Shofar.
