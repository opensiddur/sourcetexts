Prayer before the Shofar 8x‏ זף
Master of the worlds, Thy children, the children of Thy‏ רבון
favourites, turn their face towards Thee and trust in Thy‏
great mercy. They have appointed me to blow the Shofar‏
before Thee, as Thou hast commanded us in Thy Torah.‏
Remember unto them the merit of our father Abraham, who‏
walked before Thee in perfection; the merit of our father‏
Isaac and his readiness for sacrifice when he lay bound upon‏
the altar; the merit of our father Jacob, the upright man who‏
dwelt in tents, as well as the merit of Moses, Aaron, David,‏
Solomon and all the righteous men of the world. Rise from‏
the throne of justice and sit on the throne of compassion, as‏
it is said: God has arisen at the Teruah, the Lord at the sound‏
of the Shofar ... I myself know that I am not worthy to pray‏
for myself, let alone for others, all the more since I have‏
neither the knowledge nor the wisdom to bear in mind the‏
intentions underlying the Shofar sounds and their connections‏
with Thy holy names. But I trust in Thy great compassion‏
and know that Thou wilt not turn me away empty from‏
before Thee. And Thou in Thy goodness dost arouse Thine‏
own compassion. So let the favour of the Lord our God be‏
upon us and as for the work of our hands, confirm it. Consider‏
good intentions as if they had been deeds, as if we had kept‏
in mind all the intentions underlying the Shofar sounds, in‏
order that those sounds of ours may ascend to arouse Thy‏
compassion so that Thou mayest take pity on Thy children,‏
through these Shofar sounds, transform the attribute of‏
justice into the attribute of kindness and compassion and‏
deal indulgently with us. Close and seal Satan’s mouth that‏
he may not accuse us and when our reproach comes before‏
Thee, do not blot out our name from Thy book. Inscribe all‏
the children of our covenant for a good life. May the words‏
of my mouth and the meditation of my heart find favour‏
before thee, O Lord, my rock and my redeemer, Amen.‏
