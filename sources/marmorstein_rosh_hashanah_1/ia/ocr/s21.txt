(Deut. XI. 13) And it shall happen if you listen carefully
to My commandments which I command you today, to love
the Lord your God and to serve Him with all your heart and
with all your soul, that I will grant the rain of your land in
its season, the former rain and the latter rain, so that you
will gather in your corn, your wine and your oil. I will give
grass to your field for your cattle, and you shall eat and be
satisfied. Take care that your heart be not deceived and that
you do not turn aside and serve strange gods and bow down
before them. For then the wrath of the Lord will burn against
you and He will close the heavens so that no rain shall fall
and the earth shall not yield its produce, and you shall soon
perish from the good land that the Lord is giving you. Lay
these words of mine upon your heart and upon your soul, bind
them as a sign upon your arm and let them be frontlets
between your eyes. Teach them to your children by talking
about them when you sit in your house and walk on the way
and when you lie down and when you rise up. You shall
write them upon the doorposts of your house and upon your
gates, so that your days and the days of your children may
increase upon the soil which the Lord has sworn to your
fathers to give to them, as the days of the heavens above the
earth.
