52 Morning Prayer for the New Year 52
Thou art our God in heaven and on earth.‏ אתה
Mighty and awe-inspiring distinguished among myriads.‏
He spoke and it came to pass, He commanded, then were‏
His memory is forever, they created.
Pure is His gaze, He lives in all eternity.
His crown is salvation, He is enthroned in mystery.
His cloak is zeal, His robe is righteousness.
His mystery is equity, He is girded with retribution.
His work is truth, His counsel faith.
He is close to those who call He is just and upright.
Him in truth, lofty and exalted.
He resides in the clouds and suspends the earth
in space.
Living, enduring, awe-inspiring, lofty and holy.
