(Psalm 19.) For the conductor of the Choir, a Psalm by
David: The heavens tell of the glory of God, and the firma-
ment proclaims the work of His hand. Day utters speech to
day and night to night expresses knowledge. There is neither
speech nor words without their sound being heard. Their line
goes out over the whole earth and at the ends of the world
are their words, for the sun has He set a tent among them.
And he (the sun) comes forth like a bridegroom from his
canopy, rejoices like a hero to run a course. At one end of
the heavens is his starting point and his circuit goes round to
the other end — nothing can hide from his glow. The Torah
