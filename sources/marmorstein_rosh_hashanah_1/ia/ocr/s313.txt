The Priests:
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has sanctified us with the sanctity of Aaron and com-‏
manded us to bless His people Israel in love.‏
Congregation:
May the Lord bless you from Zion,
the creator of heaven and earth.
O Lord, our Master, how mighty is
Thy Name over the whole earth.
Protect me, O Lord, for I trust in
Thee.
May God be gracious to us and bless
us, may He cause His countenance to
shine with us, Selah.
The Lord, the Lord is a merciful
and gracious God, slow to anger and
great in kindness and truth.
Turn towards me and be gracious to
me for I am alone and poor.
Towards Thee, O Lord, I lift up my
soul.
Behold just as the eyes of slaves are
on the hand of their master and as
the eyes of the handmaid are on the
hand of her mistress, so are our eyes
on the Lord our God until He will
be gracious to us.
May he receive blessing from the
Lord and kindness from the God
of his salvation and find grace and
favour in the eyes of God and man.
O Lord, be gracious to us, to Thee
we hope, be their strength every
morning as well as our help in time
of trouble.
