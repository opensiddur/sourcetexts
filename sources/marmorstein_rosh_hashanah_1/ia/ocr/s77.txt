(Psalm 92.) A psalm, a song for the Sabbath day: It is good
to thank the Lord and to sing praise to Thy Name, O Most
High, to proclaim Thy kindness in the morning and Thy
faithfulness at night, on the ten-stringed harp, through med-
itation on the zither. For Thou hast gladdened me, O Lord,
with Thy deeds, in the works of Thy hands do I exult. How
great are Thy works, O Lord, profound indeed are Thy
thoughts. A simpleton cannot know, nor can a fool under-
stand this, when the wicked flourish like grass and all evil-
doers blossom forth only to be destroyed for ever. But Thou
art supreme to eternity, O Lord. Behold Thine enemies, O
Lord, behold Thine enemies shall perish, all evil-doers shall
scatter. Then shalt Thou lift up my horn like the unicorn,
I am anointed with fresh oil. Then shall my eye look down
upon my adversaries, when evil men rise up against me my
ears shall hear. The righteous shall flourish like the palm,
like the cedar on Lebanon shall he grow. Those planted in
the House of the Lord shall blossom forth in the courts of
our God. They will yet be luxuriant in old age, full of sap
and fresh will they be, so as to proclaim that the Lord is
upright, my Rock in whom there is no wrong.
(Psalm 93.) The Lord reigns, clothed in majesty, the Lord
clothes Himself in strength, He girds Himself. Surely the
world stands firm without shaking. Thy throne is established
from of old, since eternity art Thou. The streams, O Lord,
the streams have raised their voice, the streams shall raise
their roar. Mightier than many waters are the breakers of the
sea but still mightier on high is the Lord. Thy testimonies
are indeed sure, holiness becomes Thine House, O Lord, for
an infinity of days.
