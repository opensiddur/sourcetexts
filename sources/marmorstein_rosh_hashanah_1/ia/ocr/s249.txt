האוחז‎ In His hand He holds the attribute of judgment.
All believe that He is God of faith.
He tests and examines hidden secrets.
All believe that He tests the inwards.
He redeems from death and delivers from destruction.
All believe that He is a strong redeemer.
He judges alone those who come into the world.
All believe that He is judge of truth.
He is thought of as ‘I am that I am’.
All believe that He was, is and shall be.
Sure is His Name and likewise His fame.
All believe that He is with none beside Him.
He remembers those who remember Him in kind memory.
All believe that He remembers the covenant.
He distributes life to all the living.
All believe that He lives and endures.
He is good and acts well towards evil and good.
All believe that He is good towards all.
He knows the impulse of all creatures.
All believe that He has formed them in the womb.
He has power over all and contains them all.
All believe that He has power over all.
Almighty, He dwells in mystery, in shadow.
All believe that He alone is He.
He appoints kings and His is the kingdom.
All believe that He is King of the world.
He leads with His mercy every generation.
All believe that He retains kindness.
He is long suffering and hides His eye from the stubborn.
All believe that He grants pardon.
He is supreme and His eye rests on those who fear Him.
All believe that He answers soft prayer.
