The fourth of the wild beasts (that Daniel saw) will fall into
the fire, among the sparking flames at the fiery sound of the
Shofar. Thou wilt stir up sleep to pay retribution to the
oppressor, and shatter his multitudes with the mighty sound
of the Shofar. Thou wilt free the Rose (Israel) and let
redemption dawn, revive those who slumber with the rousing
sound of the Shofar. Thou wilt march from Hermon to
destroy Admon, bring cooling to Tsalmon with the massive
sound of the Shofar. Thou wilt step in our midst as once in
the wilderness when Thou didst give us the Torah and once
again wilt Thou allow to be heard in the Sanctuary a voice
that sounds like that of the Shofar.
