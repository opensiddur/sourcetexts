it was said: ‘God heard their cry, then God remembered His
covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob’. And it was said:
‘I will remember my covenant with Jacob, as well as my
covenant with Isaac and my covenant with Abraham will I
also remember, and the land will I remember.’ And in Thy
holy words it is written: ‘A memorial has He made for His
wonders, gracious and compassionate is the Lord’. And it was
said: ‘He gave food to those that fear Him, He will remember
His covenant for ever’. And it was said: ‘He remembered unto
them His covenant and He reversed the decree according to
the abundance of His kindness’. And through Thy servants
the prophets it is written saying: ‘Go and proclaim in the
ears of Jerusalem saying, ‘Thus says the Lord: I remember
unto you the grace of your youth, the love of your betrothal,
when you followed me in the wilderness in land unsown’.
And it was said: ‘I will remember My covenant with you
in the days of your youth and establish with you an everlasting
covenant’. And it was said: ‘Is Ephraim a dear son to me,
or a delightful child that whenever I speak of him, I still
remember him? Accordingly, my love for him is aroused,
I shall surely have compassion on him, says the Lord’.
