15 Evening Prayer for the New Year 15
Lead us to rest, O Lord our God, in peace, and‏ השכיבנו
let us rise, O our King, to life. Spread over us the tent of‏
Thy peace and improve us through good counsel from before‏
Thee. Help us for the sake of Thy Name, protect us and‏
remove from us foe, pestilence, sword, famine and grief.‏
Remove Satan from in front of us and behind us; and in the‏
shadow of Thy wings shelter us, for Thou art God, our‏
guardian and our deliverer, for Thou art God, a gracious and‏
merciful King. Guard our going out and our return to life‏
and peace from now to eternity. Spread over us the tent of‏
Thy peace. Blessed art Thou, O Lord, who spreads the tent‏
of peace over us and over all His people Israel and over‏
Jerusalem.‏
(On Sabbath) ושמרו‎ The Children of Israel shall keep the
Sabbath to celebrate the Sabbath throughout their generations
as an eternal covenant. Between Me and the Children of Israel
it shall be a sign for ever, that in six days the Lord created
the heavens and the earth and on the seventh day He ceased
from work and rested.
Blow the Shofar on the New Moon when the moon‏ תקעו
is covered on the day of our festival, for it is a statute for‏
Israel, a judgment for the God of Jacob.‏
The Reader says half Kaddish.
ועתה‎ And now let the power of the Lord appear great,
as Thou hast said. Remember Thy compassion, O Lord
and Thy kindnesses, for they exist since eternity.
Exalted and sanctified be His Great Name in the‏ יתנדל
world which He has created according to His will. May His‏
Kingdom be established in your lifetime and in your days‏
and in the lifetime of the whole House of Israel, speedily‏
and in a short time, and say: Amen!‏
May His Great Name be praised to eternity and the eternity
of eternities!
Blessed and praised and glorified and elevated and exalted
and adorned and raised on high and lauded be the Name of
the Holy One, blessed be He; high, yea high, above all
blessing and song, praise and comfort that have ever been
spoken in the world, and say Amen!
