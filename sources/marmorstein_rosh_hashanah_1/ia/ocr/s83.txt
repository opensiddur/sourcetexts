(Psalm 147.) Halleluyah. For the Lord is good, sing to our
God for He is pleasant, praise is becoming. The builder of
Jerusalem is the Lord, the dispersed of Israel will He gather
together. It is He who heals the broken-hearted and binds
their wounds. He counts the number of the stars and calls
all of them by name. Great is our Lord, abundant of power,
His understanding is beyond measure. A supporter of the
humble is the Lord, He casts down the wicked to the ground.
Respond to the Lord with gratitude, play to our God upon
the harp. He who covers heaven with clouds, who prepares
rain for the earth, who makes grass grow on mountains, He
gives the beast its food, to the young ravens when they call
out. He delights not in the might of the horse nor takes
pleasure in the legs of a man. The Lord takes pleasure in
those who fear Him, in those who wait for His grace. Extol
the Lord, O Jerusalem, praise your God, O Zion. For He has
strengthened the breaches of your gates, He has blessed your
children in your midst. Peace has He placed in your border
and satisfied you with rich wheat. He sends forth His com-
mand upon the earth, very swiftly does His word run. He
grants snow like wool and scatters frost like dust. He hurls
down His ice like morsels of bread, who can stand before
His cold. He sends His word to melt them, causes His wind
to blow and water flows. He tells His words to Jacob, His
statutes and judgments to Israel. He did not do this to any
other nation, they do not know His judgments. Halleluyah.
