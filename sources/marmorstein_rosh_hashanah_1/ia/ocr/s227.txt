3 Musaph for the New Year 3
Who is like Thee, Father of compassion who remembers‏ מי
His creatures for life in compassion; and Thou art faithful‏
to revive the dead. Blessed art Thou, O Lord, who revives‏
the dead.‏
Even on the path of Thy judgments, O Lord, we‏ אף ארח
trust in Thee; O Thou, longing of our heart, in adversity we‏
search for Thee; pure of heart, we appeared already yesterday‏
before Thee, even before sounding the tone of the Shofar. By‏
decree didst Thou decide from the original creation to appoint‏
repentance as the first of Thy pronouncements; even before‏
the first judgment was arranged, repentance was already there‏
to save sinners from destruction. Thou who hast formed their‏
two instincts together and seest and knowest their innermost‏
thoughts, should a sin appear within their hiding places, Thou‏
wilt surely bear their great ancestors in mind for them.‏
Remember him (Joseph) whose shoulder Thou didst relieve‏
from the burden, who as a slave, was freed from the affliction‏
of fetters; though his offsprings have erred through lust, may‏
his purity save them in this life of vanity. As for the day‏
hidden in Thy heart and the retribution at the end of days,‏
bring about the gathering of the exiles, the day prepared for‏
the end of seasons, that is awaited from year to year, from‏
time to time. Reveal the hidden day, decide the month for‏
the severed thorns (sinners) to be burnt and disappear, for‏
the stumbling block to be removed from the crooked heart‏
and for Abraham, Isaac and Jacob to be remembered.‏
The Lord will reign for ever, your God, O Zion, from‏ ימלך
generation to generation. Halleluyah.‏
And Thou, O Holy One, art enthroned above the‏ ואתה
songs of praise of Israel, O God.‏
