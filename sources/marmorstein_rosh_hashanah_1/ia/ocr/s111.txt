41 Morning Prayer for the New Year 41
Thee and whom Thou didst teach the statutes of life, likewise
be gracious to us and teach us. Our Father, merciful and
compassionate Father, have compassion upon us and set it
in our heart to comprehend and to understand, to hear, to
learn and to teach, to keep, perform and fulfil all words of
the teaching of Thy Torah in love. Enlighten our eyes through
Thy Torah, attach our heart to Thy commandments, conse-
crate our heart to love Thy Name and to fear it, so that never
may we be disgraced. For in Thy great and awe-inspiring
Name do we trust, we exult and rejoice in Thy help. Bring
us in peace from the four corners of the earth and lead us
upright into our land. For Thou art God who creates salvation
and Thou hast chosen us from all peoples and tongues and
brought us near Thy great Name for ever in truth, to thank
Thee and to proclaim Thy unity. Blessed art Thou, O Lord,
who has chosen His people Israel in love.
(Deut. VI. 4)
Hear, O Israel, the Lord is our God, the Lord is One.
Blessed be His glorious royal Name for ever and ever.
