34 Morning Prayer for the New Year 34
May Thy Name be praised for ever, O our King,‏ ישתבח
God the great and holy King in heaven and on earth. For‏
Thine, O Lord our God and God of our fathers, are song and‏
glory, praise and music, might and dominion, victory, great-‏
ness and strength, fame and splendour, blessings and thanks‏
from now until eternity. Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, King‏
famed in eulogies, God of thanksgiving, Master of wonders,‏
who chooses songs of praise, O King, everlasting God.‏
The reader recites half Kaddish.
ועתה‎ And now let the power of the Lord appear great,
as Thou hast said: Remember Thy mercy, O Lord, and
Thy kindnesses for they have always existed.
Exalted and sanctified be His Great Name in the‏ יתנדל
world which He has created according to His will. May His‏
Kingdom be established in your lifetime and in your days‏
and in the lifetime of the whole House of Israel, speedily‏
and in a short time, and say: Amen!‏
May His Great Name be praised to eternity and the eternity
of eternities.
Blessed and praised and glorified and elevated and exalted
and adorned and raised on high and lauded be the Name of
the Holy One, blessed be He; high, yea high, above all
blessing and song, praise and comfort that has ever been
spoken in the world, and say Amen.
Congregation:
Blessed and praised, glorified,
exalted and extolled be the Name
of the Supreme King of Kings,
the Holy One blessed be He. He
is the first and He is the last and
beside Him there is no God.
Make way before Him, who is
enthroned above the clouds, Yah
is His Name, and rejoice before
Him, His Name is exalted above
all blessing and fame. Blessed be
Him, His Name is exalted above
and ever. Let the Lord’s Name
be blessed from now until eter-
nity.
