36 Morning Prayer for the New Year 36
King, who rules the world in His might, mountains quake
before His majesty and jump like rams before His rebuke.
The King, awe-inspiring to the kings of the earth, the earth
is surely quaking before Him who dwells above the Cherubim,
the earth trembles. The King, who has the strength to bear
Him? For He bears all with strength and to the weary does
He grant strength. The King, when He rises to give judgment
on the day of judgment, to judge the proud in judgment. The
King searches out the secret of those who go deep to conceal
a plan in the depths, and He reveals and exposes the things
that are deep. O King, command that the haughty tyrants
should be hemmed in from every side by the fury of the
tempest. The King assembles the kings of the earth when
He fulfils in the storm the prophesy of Duma, then will He
reckon with the host of the summit. The King, high and
exalted in judgment, the might of the King loves judgment,
the foundation of His throne is justice and judgment. The
King is the just Judge, justice walks before Him, to plead
for those who strive for justice. The King mighty in dominion,
His throne is suspended above, His realm prevails over all.
The King makes the earth quake with His glance, He shakes
the foundations with His glance, over all there roams His
glance. The King searches every creature, and all that he may
do both below and above.
The King is God of eternity, the eternal people
has crowned Him. The Lord rules unto eternity,
the Holy One.
