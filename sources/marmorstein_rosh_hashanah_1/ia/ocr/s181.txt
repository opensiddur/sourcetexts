at Shiloh. The lad was still very young. They slaughtered the
bullock, and brought the lad to Eli. She said: ‘I pray you,
my lord, as your soul lives, my lord, I am the woman who
was standing here to pray to the Lord. For this lad did I pray
and the Lord granted my request for which I asked of Him.
I then dedicated him to the Lord; all the days that he exists,
he is dedicated to the Lord’. Then he bowed down there
before the Lord. And Hannah prayed and said: ‘My heart
has rejoiced in the Lord, my condition has been raised through
the Lord; my mouth has triumphed over my enemies since
I have rejoiced in Thy salvation. None is holy as the Lord
for there is none besides Thee and there is no rock like our
God. Do not continue to talk haughtily, let insolence leave
your mouth for a God of knowledge is the Lord and to Him
all deeds are known. The bow of the mighty breaks and
stumblers are girded with strength, those once satisfied with
food hire themselves out ana the hungry cease to be hungry,
even she who was childless has given birth to seven while
another rich in children is forlorn. The Lord slays and revives,
brings down to the grave and raises up. The Lord impover-
