IOI Musaph for the New Year IOL
From of old has this day been appointed for judgment,‏ אפר
for testing every day’s work; the creatures approach the‏
countenance of the Awe-inspiring One that on it He may‏
grant them a verdict of deliverance. On this day was the first‏
man created; he received a law but did not keep it; on this‏
day did he find a defender (in God) who helped him in need‏
and appointed it a day of judgment to be maintained for‏
later generations. On this day the hewn out foundations, the‏
hills and the rocks, were formed for the sake of the first of‏
the forefathers; and they, as dwellers in the garden (Eden)‏
were present at the work of creation that on this day they‏
might plead for those created. This day’s month bears the‏
name of the immortals (forefathers); it is a sign for those‏
above and below to assemble for the books to be opened,‏
for deeds to be related and for men to pass by Thee and‏
render account. This day was appointed first for the order‏
of Thy festivals, and for the flock to be led under the staff‏
past Thee; when Thy witnesses blow (remember) the Shofar‏
today, remember, O Merciful One, the oath of Thy servants.
The sound of the Shofar rises with prayer, Almighty,‏ נעלה
to appease Thee with supplication; return the flashing sword‏
to its sheath, grasp the shield that we may shelter beneath it.
Remember us for life, O King, who takes delight in‏ זכרנו
life and inscribe us in the book of life for Thine own sake,‏
O living God, King, helper, saviour and shield. Blessed art‏
Thou, O Lord, shield of Abraham.‏
