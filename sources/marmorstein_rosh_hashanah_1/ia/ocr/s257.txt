118 Musaph for the New Year 118
The Holy Ark is opened.
It is right for us to praise the Master of everything,‏ עלינו
and ascribe greatness to the Creator of the beginning, that‏
He has not made us like the nations of the lands and has not‏
placed us like the families of the earth, that He has not made‏
our portion like theirs nor our destiny like that of all their‏
multitude. We kneel, bow down and give thanks before the‏
Supreme King of kings, the Holy One blessed be He, who‏
has extended the heavens and founded the earth and His‏
glorious dwelling is in Heaven above and His mighty presence‏
in the loftiest heights. He is our God, there is no other, In‏
truth He is our King, there is none besides Him. As it is‏
written in His Torah: ‘And you shall know this day and‏
repeat it in your heart that the Lord is God in the heavens‏
above and that on earth below there is no other.’‏
You were taught to perceive that the Lord is God‏ אתה
with none besides Him. You shall know this day and repeat‏
it in your heart that the Lord is God in the heavens above‏
and that on earth below there is no other. Hear, O Israel,‏
the Lord is our God, the Lord is One. Behold to the Lord‏
your God heaven and the highest heavens belong, the earth‏
and all that is on it. For the Lord your God is the supreme‏
God and the supreme Lord, the great, mighty and awe-‏
inspiring God, who neither respects rank nor accepts bribes.‏
For I call upon the Name of the Lord, ascribe greatness to‏
our God. May the Name of the Lord be blessed from now‏
until eternity.‏
The Holy Ark is closed.
