He opens the gates for those who knock in repentance.
All believe that His hand is open.
He waits for the evil-doer and delights in his justification.
All believe that He is righteous and upright.
He shortens anger and holds back wrath.
All believe that He is hard to anger.
Compassionate, He lets mercy precede wrath.
All believe that He is soft to appease.
He is just and treats small and great equally.
All believe that He is a righteous judge.
He is perfect and shows perfection with the flawless.
All believe that His work is flawless.
