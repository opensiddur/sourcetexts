14 Musaph for the New Year I44
s/s Our God and God of our fathers, blow the great
Shofar for our freedom and raise a banner for the gathering
of our exiles and bring near our dispersed ones from the
corners of the earth and bring us to Zion Thy city in mirth
and to Jerusalem Thy sanctuary in eternal joy and there we
will perform before Thee our sacrificial obligations according
to the commandment given to us in Thy Torah through Thy
servant Moses from the mouth of Thy glory as it is said:
‘and on the day of your rejoicing and on your festivals and
on your new moons, you shall blow trumpets over your
whole-offerings and over the sacrifices of your peace-offerings
and they shall be a reminder on your behalf before your God,
I am the Lord your God’. For Thou dost hear the sound of
the Shofar and listen to Teruah and there is none like Thee.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord, who hearest the Teruah sound of
His people Israel in compassion.
The Shofar is blown (but not on the Sabbath).
