In the book of life, blessing, peace and a good‏ בספר
livelihood, may we be remembered and inscribed before Thee,‏
we and all Thy people the house of Israel for a good life and‏
for peace. Blessed art Thou, O Lord who makes peace.‏
My God, guard my tongue from evil and my lips from‏ אלהי
speaking falsehood. Towards those who curse me, let my soul‏
be silent and let my soul be as dust in relation to everything.‏
Open my heart to Thy Torah and let my soul pursue Thy‏
commandments and as for all those that devise evil against‏
me, speedily frustrate their counsel and upset their plan. Do‏
this for the sake of Thy Name, do this for the sake of Thy
right hand, do this for the sake of Thy holiness, do this for
the sake of Thy Torah so that those who love Thee may be
delivered. Save with Thy right hand and respond to me. May
the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart find
favour before Thee, O Lord, my Rock and my Redeemer.
May He who makes peace in His heights, make peace for us
and for all Israel, and say, Amen.
May it find favour before Thee, O Lord our God and God of‏ יהי
our fathers, that the sanctuary be rebuilt speedily in our days, and‏
grant our portion in Thy Torah. There we will serve Thee in awe as‏
in the days of old and former years. And pleasant unto the Lord shall‏
be the offering of Judah and Jerusalem as in the days of old and‏
former years.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God and God of our‏ ברוך

great, mighty and awe-inspiring God; God the most high‏
who performs acts of kindness and owns everything and‏
remembers the good deeds of the fathers and will bring a‏
redeemer for their children’s children for His Name’s sake‏
in love.‏
Choosing from the counsel of wise and discerning men‏ מסור
