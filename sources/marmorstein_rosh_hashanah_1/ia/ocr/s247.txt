3 Musaph for the New Year 113
One is our God, He is our father, He is our king, He is‏ אחר
our helper; He will again let us hear in His mercy before the‏
eyes of all the living: ‘To be God unto you’.‏
I am the Lord your God.‏ אני
O Mighty One, our Mighty One, O Lord, our Lord, how‏ אדיר
mighty is Thy Name over the whole earth. The Lord will be King over‏
the whole earth, on that day will the Lord be One and His Name One.‏
The fame of Thine honour makes Thee feared upon‏ תהלות
the whole earth. When Thou didst make judgment heard‏
from heaven, the earth shook before it returned to its rest.‏
Before the storm on the day of judgment, the floods of the‏
earth roared. There was a mighty Shofar blast, then the Lord‏
spoke and called to the earth, the end of the earth proclaimed‏
the beauty of the righteous God in a song of praise. Fear,‏
fright, dread and terror will then take hold of the earth when‏
Thou arisest for judgment, to show anger with all the ends‏
of the earth. ‘Make straight, clear the way’ those who tremble‏
at the earth’s shaking will say. O luminous and mighty One,‏
Thou wilt reassure the earth: ‘what do you fear, O earth, let‏
the depths of the earth rejoice’.‏
That it may be known and revealed that He is King‏ להתודע
over the whole earth; just as He was always praised in heaven,‏
so shall His tested ones acquire the right to praise Him on‏
earth.‏
