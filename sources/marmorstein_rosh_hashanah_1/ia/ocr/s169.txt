73 Torah Reading for the New Year 73
Before reading from the Torah, the individual called upon
says the following:
Bless the Lord who is called blessed.‏ ברכו
The congregation responds:
Blessed is the Lord who is called blessed,‏ ברוך
for ever and ever.‏
He then repeats this and continues:
ברוך‎ Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who has chosen us from all peoples and given us His Torah.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord, giver of the Torah.
Then the reading of the Torah begins until the end of the portion
when the individual who had been called upon, says:
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has given us the Torah of truth and implanted in us‏
eternal life. Blessed art Thou, O Lord, giver of the Torah.‏
Reading from the First Torah Scroll
(Genesis: Chapter 21)

did for Sarah just as He had spoken. And Sarah conceived

Isaac. Abraham circumcised his son Isaac when he was eight
days old just as God had commanded him. Abraham was a
