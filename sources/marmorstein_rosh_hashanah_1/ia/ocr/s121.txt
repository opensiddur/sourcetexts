46 Morning Prayer for the New Year 46
Our God and God of our fathers, may there rise‏ אלהינו
and come and arrive and appear and be favourably received‏
and be heard and considered and remain in memory before‏
Thee, our remembrance and our consideration and the mem-‏
ory of our fathers and the memory of the anointed, the son of‏
David, Thy servant, and the memory of Jerusalem the Holy‏
City, and the memory of all Thy people, the House of Israel,‏
for deliverance and for happiness, for favour and for grace‏
and for compassion, for life and for peace on this Day of‏
Remembrance. Remember us, O Lord our God, on it for‏
happiness, consider us on it for blessing and help us on it‏
unto life, and through the word of salvation and compassion,‏
spare us and be gracious unto us have compassion upon us,‏
and help us, for towards Thee our eyes are turned, for Thou‏
O God, art a gracious and merciful King.‏
Our God and God of our fathers, reign over the whole‏ או'א
world in Thy glory and rise above the whole earth in Thy‏
grandeur. Shine forth in the exalted beauty of Thy might‏
upon all the inhabitants of the territory of Thine earth so‏
that every creature may recognize that Thou hast created it‏
and that every form may understand that Thou hast formed‏
it and all with breath in the nostrils may say: The Lord, the‏
God of Israel, is King and His Kingdom rules over all. (Our‏
God and God of our fathers, take pleasure in our rest.)‏
Sanctify us through Thy commandments and grant our portion‏
in Thy Torah, satisfy us with Thy goodness and gladden us‏
with Thy help (and grant us, O Lord our God, in love and‏
favour, Thy holy Sabbath as a portion so that Israel, who‏
sanctifies Thy Name, may rest on it) and purify our heart to‏
serve Thee in truth for Thou, O Lord, art truth and Thy word‏
is truth and endures for ever. Blessed art Thou, O Lord King‏
over the whole earth, who sanctifies (the Sabbath and) Israel‏
and the Day of Remembrance.‏
Take delight, O Lord our God, in Thy people Israel‏ רצה
and in their prayer and restore the service to the sanctuary‏
of Thy House and accept with love and favour the fire‏
offerings of Israel and their prayer, and may the service of‏
Thy people Israel be a perpetual delight.‏
