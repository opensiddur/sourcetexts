For Thou art our God, O King.‏
20 The King sustains the earth in justice. He is supreme
over all the earth. The whole earth acknowledges and knows,
all inhabitants of the world and dwellers on earth, from one
end of the earth to the other, that He is Creator of the ends
of all the earth who does justice and righteousness on earth.
When for justice He rises in court — the earth is silent — to
save all the humble of the earth, to make as nought the judges
of the earth, to grasp the corners of the earth and throw off
all the evil-doers of the earth. Then aloud do they proclaim
in heaven and on earth: “How mighty is Thy Name through-
out all the earth.” When He rules over all the earth, the
depths of the earth shout with joy, the heavens rejoice and
the earth exults together with what is in the heavens above
the earth and what is in the waters below the earth. Then
do they joyfully shout in adoration: ‘All the earth is full of
His glory”. The Lord reigns, the earth rejoices. Three times
will He let the Shofar blast be heard in dread and terrify the
princes of dwellers on earth, and He will overthrow the
thrones of the kingdoms of the earth so that all with breath
in their nostrils on earth shall say: “How mighty is Thy Name
throughout all the earth.” He establishes joy on the whole
earth, arouses all who sleep in the earth, and they will lift
up hymns of praise from the corner of the earth: “sing to
the Lord, all the earth’.
