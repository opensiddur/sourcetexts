those who bear the heavy yoke, then He remembers as an

remembers the foundation stones (forefathers), who groan
in Egyptian slavery. The sucklings who praised the Lord at
the Sea of Reeds, the promise of the covenant (the circum-
cision) and the statutes (of the Torah), His holy word, all
these does He remember. The memory of those who live
austerely (students of the Torah) and study His beautiful
sayings, who with their lips provide a substitute for sacrificial
bullocks, He does remember. When the books that announce
what is hidden, are examined, He remembers in love the
wonderful pair of brothers. He remembers the honour of the
sanctuary, the place where He, the Beloved, did dwell, where
He allowed the Cherubim to rest, the love of our youth He
remembers. The basin on its frame, the delightful voice (of
the Levites) on the Temple steps, the Almighty recalls them
in favour of the discerning on the appointed day of judgment.

for remembrance. O Lord, in Thee do I trust and not in
princes, for they are in the grave and Thy Name is for ever
for remembrance. All these supported me but I seek Thy
compassion, without it I should have perished and not
survived for remembrance. For in spite of their strength,
they could not plead for me before Thee, those in whose
mouths is Thy greatness have placed it there for remembrance.
For this day has been stored the sum of assessments, it is the
beginning of days and the first for remembrance. On it there
is read out what was engraved with a diamond quill, what
was revealed and explained and known in remembrance.
Death and life, peace and war, dearth and abundance, appear
for remembrance. The acts of man, the number of his steps,
though forgotten by men, they are to God for remembrance.
“My way is hidden”? — who could say that? In vain was a
sin inscribed for me for remembrance. In front of man his
deeds give evidence, his falsehood bears witness against him
for remembrance. Proclaim the plans of the Lord, all men
together, pass under the staff like a flock for remembrance.
