the day that Elkanah sacrificed, he used to give portions to
his wife Peninah and to all her sons and daughters but to
Hannah he used to give a double portion because he loved
Hannah even though the Lord had closed her womb. Her rival
used to annoy her by taunting her that the Lord had closed her
womb. He used to do this year by year and whenever she went
up to the house of the Lord, her rival would annoy her so that
she used to weep and would not eat. Once her husband Elkanah
said to her: ‘Hannah, why do you weep, why do you not eat
and why is your heart sad? Am I not better to you than ten
children?’ Hannah arose after eating and drinking in Shiloh:
and Eli the priest was sitting on a chair by the doorpost of
the Sanctuary of the Lord when she in bitterness of soul
prayed to the Lord and was weeping violently. And she made
a vow and said: ‘O Lord of Hosts, if Thou wilt only look at
the misery of Thy handmaid and remember me and not forget
Thy handmaid and give Thy handmaid a male child, I will
give him to the Lord all the days of his life and no razor shall
come upon his head’. And it happened when she prayed so
much before the Lord that Eli noticed her mouth. For Hannah
was talking to herself, only her lips were moving but her
voice could not be heard. So Eli thought that she was drunk
and Eli said to her: ‘How much longer will you be drunk?
