(Psalm 34.) By David when he disguised his sanity before
Abimelech, who drove him away and he went: I will at all
times bless the Lord, His fame shall always be in my mouth.
My soul glories in the Lord, let the humble hear and rejoice.
Magnify the Lord with me and let us exalt His Name together.
I sought the Lord, and He answered me and saved me from
all my terrors. When they look to Him, they are radiant and
their faces are not shamed. This man called and the Lord
hears and from all his troubles did He save him. An angel of
the Lord encamps around those that fear Him and rescued
them. Observe and perceive that the Lord is good, fortunate
the man who trusts in Him. Fear the Lord, O His holy ones,
for there is no want among those that fear Him. Though
whelps may hunger and starve, those that seek the Lord will
not lack all that is good. Come, children, listen to me, the
fear of the Lord will I teach you. Who is the man that desires
life, who longs for days to see goodness? Keep your tongue
from evil and your lips from speaking guile, depart from
evil and do good, seek peace and pursue it. The eyes of the
Lord are towards the righteous and His ears to their cry.
