67 Abinu Malkenu 67
On Sabbath אבינו מלבנו‎ is not recited.
The Holy Ark is opened.
אבינו‎ Our Father, our King, we have sinned before Thee.
Our Father, our King, we have no king but Thee.
Our Father, our King, deal with us kindly for the sake of
Thy Name.
Our Father, our King, renew for us a good year.
Our Father, our King, annul all harsh decrees from upon us.
Our Father, our King, annul the plans of those that hate us.
Our Father, our King, frustrate the counsel of our enemies.
Our Father, our King, avert every foe and adversary from
upon us.
Our Father, our King, avert pestilence, sword, famine, cap-
tivity and destruction from the chil-
dren of Thy covenant.
Our Father, our King, hold back plague from Thine inher-
itance.
Our Father, our King, send complete healing to the sick of
Thy people.
Our Father, our King, restore us in complete repentance
before Thee.
Our Father, our King, pardon and forgive all our sins.
Our Father, our King, blot out our transgressions and let
them pass away from before Thine
eyes.
Our Father, our King, tear up the severe verdict decreed
against us.
Our Father, our King, erase in Thy great compassion all the
records of our faults.
Our Father, our King, remember that we are dust.
Our Father, our King, remember us benevolently before
Thee.
Our Father, our King, inscribe us in the book of good life.
