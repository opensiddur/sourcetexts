The Lord will reign for ever and ever.‏

giance aloud: The Lord is King.
Those formed in lightning
bless Thee aloud: The Lord has reigned.

The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.

aloud: The Lord is King.
