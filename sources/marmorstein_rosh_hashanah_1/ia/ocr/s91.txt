swallowed them. By Thy grace hast Thou led this people

has seized the inhabitants of Philistia. Then have the rulers
of Edom been terrified, as for the notables of Moab, fright
seizes them, all inhabitants of Canaan have melted with fear.

Thine arm let them be silent as the stone until Thy people
pass, O Lord, until this people whom Thou hast acquired
shall pass. Thou wilt bring them and plant them upon the
mount of Thine inheritance, the site for Thy dwelling which
Thou hast made, O Lord, the sanctuary, O Lord, which Thy
hands have established. The Lord will reign for ever and
ever. The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
