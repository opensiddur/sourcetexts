audible that He may remember them kindly. He remembers
the sacrifice on Moriah (Isaac) whose eyes become dim; for
the sake of people naked and bare, may He recall the ram
caught in the thicket. When the fruitful people is afflicted
with a festering wound, may his prayer be manifest that God
may remember his merit. He remembers him who saw the
ladder in the dream (Jacob); above it the Creator appeared
to him, showed him the princes of the four empires and their
yoke. He will remember him, as in former days. When His
