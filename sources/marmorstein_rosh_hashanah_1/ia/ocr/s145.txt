58 Morning Prayer for the New Year 58
The Lord will reign.
The Seraphim around Him
glorify aloud:
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
The Lord is King.
The Lord has reigned.
The Lord will reign.
Those who classify the law
respond aloud:
Those in dread at Thy won-
ders utter aloud:
The hosts of Thy flock let it
ring out aloud:
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
The Lord is King.
The Lord has reigned.
The Lord will reign.
Holy communities sanctify
aloud:
Myriads of myriads exult
aloud:
Those fashioned of flames
repeat aloud:
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
The Lord is King.
The Lord has reigned.
The Lord will reign.
Those who maintain praises
say constantly aloud:
Those who affirm Thy glory
speak sincerely and aloud:
The perfect in testimony re-
late aloud:
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
