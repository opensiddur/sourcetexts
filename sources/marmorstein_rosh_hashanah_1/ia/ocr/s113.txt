(Numbers XV. 37) Thus spoke the Lord to Moses: Speak
to the children of Israel and tell them to make themselves
show-fringes upon the corners of their garments throughout
their generations and to fasten to the fringe of each corner
a thread of skyblue wool. They shall be show-fringes to you
so that when you look upon them, you will remember all the
commandments of the Lord and fulfil them. And do not
follow your heart and your eyes after which you go astray
Thus may you remember and fulfil all my commandments
and be holy unto your God. I am the Lord your God who
led you out of the land of Egypt in order to be your God.
