Sihon king of the Amorites
for His grace endures for ever
Og king of Bashan
for His grace endures for ever
And He gave their land as an inheritance
for His grace endures for ever
As an inheritance to Israel His servant
for His grace endures for ever
In our distress He remembered us
for His grace endures for ever

Thank the God of Heaven
for His grace endures for ever
(Psalm 33.) Exult, O righteous ones, in the Lord, it is
becoming to the upright to give praise. Thank the Lord with
the zither, with the ten-stringed harp make music to Him.
Sing to Him a new song, pluck the strings well in triumph.
For upright is the word of the Lord and all His work is with
faithfulness. He loves righteousness and justice, the grace of
the Lord fills the earth. Through the word of the Lord were
the heavens made and through the breath of His mouth all
their host. He gathers like a heap the waters of the seas, and
puts the depths into store-chambers. The whole earth is afraid
before the Lord, before Him do all inhabitants of the world
tremble. For He spoke and it was, He commanded and there
it stood. The Lord has frustrated the plan of nations, baffled
the thoughts of peoples. The counsel of the Lord endures for
ever, the thoughts of His heart from generation to generation.
Fortunate the nation whose God is the Lord, the people which
He has chosen as His inheritance. The Lord has looked from
heaven, He has seen all the sons of man. From His dwelling
place does He watch over all the inhabitants of the earth.
He who has formed their hearts altogether, He understands
all their deeds. The king is not saved by the size of an army,
the warrior will not escape through the measure of strength.
Useless is the horse for victory, by the measure of its might
