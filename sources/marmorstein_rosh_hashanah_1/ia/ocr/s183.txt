Blessings after the Haphtorah:
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
Rock of all eternity, just in all generations, faithful God who‏
says and acts, speaks and fulfils, for all His words are truth‏
and righteousness.‏
Faithful art Thou, O Lord our God and faithful are‏ נאמן
Thy words and not one of Thy words turns back empty; for‏
Thou art a faithful and compassionate God and King. Blessed‏
art Thou, O Lord God who is faithful in all His words.‏
Have mercy upon Zion, it is the centre of our life; and‏ רחם
save her, whose soul is afflicted, speedily in our days. Blessed‏
art Thou, O Lord, who makes Zion rejoice with her children.‏
Let us rejoice, O Lord our God, with the prophet‏ שמחנו
Elijah Thy servant and with the realm of the House of David‏
thine anointed. Speedily may he come that our heart may‏
have joy. Let no stranger sit on his throne, and let others no‏
longer inherit his glory for by Thy Holy Name hast Thou‏
sworn to him that his light shall not be extinguished for ever.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord, shield of David.‏
For the Torah, for the service of God, for the‏ על התורה
prophets and for this (Sabbath day and this) day of memorial,‏
which Thou, O Lord our God, hast given us, (for holiness and‏
rest) for honour and glory, for all these, O Lord our God,‏
do we thank Thee and bless Thee. Blessed be Thy name in‏
the mouth of all the living constantly and for ever and ever,‏
Thy word is truth and endures to eternity. Blessed art Thou,‏
O Lord, King over all the earth who sanctifies (the Sabbath)‏
Israel and the day of memorial.‏
After the Haphtora the Shofar is blown. But when the first day
of the festival falls on a Sabbath, then one continues immediately
with יקום פורקן‎ (P. 85)
