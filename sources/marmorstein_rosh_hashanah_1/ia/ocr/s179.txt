Lord, then they returned and arrived at their house in Ramah.
And Elkanah knew his wife Hannah and the Lord remem-
bered her; and in the course of time Hannah conceived and
gave birth to a son. She called his name Samuel ‘for I have
asked for him of the Lord’. And the man Elkanah and the
whole of his household went up to offer the annual sacrifice
and his vow to the Lord. Hannah, however, did not go up,
for she said to her husband: ‘When the lad has been weaned,
then I will bring him there that he may appear before the
presence of the Lord and stay there for ever’. Her husband
Elkanah said to her: ‘Do what is best in your eyes, stay until
you have weaned him; surely the Lord will 101 His word’.
The woman stayed and suckled her son until she had weaned
him. Then she brought him up with her after she had weaned
him, together with three bullocks, one ephah of flour and one
