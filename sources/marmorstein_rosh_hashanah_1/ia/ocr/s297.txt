all the sounds that are heard in assemblies, He has made
incumbent on communities the sound of the blast of the
Shofar. They who planned crooked deeds, deserving of death
by stoning, trembled when the cedars broke at the report of
the sound of the Shofar (on the day of the Revelation). The
Eternal of every year has set apart this day from the whole

voice’. I searched for Him in the desert and they said that
He is not there, yet when of old He gave His law, He
