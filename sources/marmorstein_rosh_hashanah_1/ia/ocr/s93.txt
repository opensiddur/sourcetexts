mows Let the breath of all the living praise Thy Name, O
Lord our God, and the spirit of all flesh glorify and exalt
Thy memory, O our King, for ever. From eternity to eternity
Thou art God and besides Thee we have no King, Redeemer,
Helper, Deliverer, Liberator and Provider, nor one to show
compassion at every time of trouble and distress. We have
no King besides Thee, O God of the first and the last, God
of all creatures, Master of all generations, who is praised by
an abundance of eulogies, who conducts His world with grace
and His creatures with compassion. The Lord neither slum-
bers nor sleeps, He wakes sleepers and arouses slumberers,
causes the dumb to speak, frees the bound, supports the
falling and straightens the bent. We give thanks to Thee
alone. Were our mouth as full of song as the sea and our
tongue of exultation as the roar of the waves and our lips
of praise as the expanse of the sky and our eyes as radiant
as the sun and the moon and our hands spread out as wide
as the eagles of heaven and our feet as swift as deer, we
should still be unable to thank Thee enough, O Lord our
God and God of our fathers, and to bless Thy Name for even
one of the many thousands and myriads of acts of kindness
which Thou hast done for our fathers and ourselves. From
Egypt hast Thou redeemed us, O Lord our God, and from
the house of slaves hast Thou freed us. Thou hast fed us in
famine and preserved us in abundance, saved us from the
sword, let us escape from plague and raised us from severe and
persistent illnesses. Up to now have Thy mercies aided us and
Thy kindnesses not forsaken us, do not abandon us, O Lord
our God, for ever. Therefore the limbs which Thou hast al-
