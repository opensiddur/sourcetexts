And so, O Lord our God, let the words of Thy Torah‏ והערב
be pleasing in our mouth and in the mouth of Thy people‏
the House of Israel so that we and our descendants and the‏
descendants of Thy people the House of Israel may all‏
acknowledge Thy Name and learn Thy Torah (with pure‏
intent). Blessed art Thou, O Lord, who teaches the Torah‏
to His people Israel .‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has chosen us from all peoples and given us His Torah.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord, the giver of the Torah.‏
May the Lord bless you and protect you. May the‏ יברכך
Lord cause His countenance to shine on you and be gracious‏
to you. May the Lord raise His countenance towards you and‏
grant you peace.‏
The following matters have no legal measure:‏ אלו
The corner of the field, the first fruits, the pilgrimage,
the practice of charity and study of the Torah. With regard
to the following matters man receives an income in this world
and the capital is stored for him in the future world: hon-
ouring father and mother the practice of charity, early atten-
dance in the House of Study mornings and evenings, hospital-
ity towards strangers, care of the sick, endowment of a bride,
escorting the dead, concentration during prayer, peace making
between man and his fellow man with the study of Torah
outweighing all of them.
My God, the soul which Thou hast given me is pure.‏ אלהי
Thou didst create it, Thou didst form it, Thou didst breathe‏
it into me and preserve it within me. Thou wilt eventually‏
take it from me and restore it to me in the future world. As‏
long as the soul is within me I thank Thee, O Lord my God,‏
and God of my fathers, Lord of all works, Master of all souls.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord, who returns souls to dead bodies.‏
