66 Morning Prayer for the New Year
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All precious in beauty force-
fully relate: The Lord is King.
All dwellers in settlement in
unison proclaim His unity: The Lord has reigned.
These and those in glory
glorify: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All the heavenly hosts in-
structively teach: The Lord is King.
All crowned with the diadem
(the Torah) in righteous-
ness justify Him: The Lord has reigned.
These and those repeat with
strength: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All mighty armies entreat in
dread: The Lord is King.
All admirers of loveliness
powerfully confirm: The Lord has reigned.
These and those sing melodi-
ously: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All saints of the Holy One
sanctify in holiness: The Lord is King.
All gathered in community
affirm in truth: The Lord has reigned.
These and those praise de-
lightfully: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All glittering Hashmalim an-
nounce afresh each morn-
ing: The Lord is King.
All Tarshishim of loftiness
whisper softly: The Lord has reigned.
These and those three times
declaim: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
