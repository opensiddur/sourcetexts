snaw I have looked again beneath the sun and found nought
better for man than preparation to cling to the fear of His
might and not to swerve. My eye has seen the course of
justice. We can discern it at the beginning when the first of
mankind was created in God’s image in the Garden of Eden.
From the time he disobeyed the mouth of the Kingdom, in
glory he no longer rested but was driven out in banishment
with the revolving blade of the sword. On the path of
goodness was (Abraham) led for his advantage, from the
east did he dispel the gloom of his own darkness, right-
eousness guided his foot. When his trust was tested, he asked
for a sign that he would possess the land and for this his
descendants were forced into exile for four hundred years.
Rise, noble men, and conquer desire, seek righteousness, seck
humility, perhaps there is hope. The pious one (Isaac) who
was led upon one of the mountains (for a sacrifice), fulfilled
on his eighth day the covenant of the flesh and sowed and
reaped a hundred fold. At sixty he begat children, one tor-
mented and one delighted him, and in old age his light became
too dim to gaze because he had favoured the evil one. There-
