(Jacob) whose tents were pleasant, whose form was engraved
on the Throne of Heaven, behold the Lord appeared to him.
When he returned from Padan and was saved from suffering
and delayed his vow before God who had appeared to him,
he suffered through Dinah, the daughter of Leah. Remember,
O creatures, to strive for His compassion, fear the storm of
the fierceness of His anger, perhaps you will find shelter on
the day of wrath. The beloved brothers, the anointed (Moses
and Aaron), who fleet as the deer made haste in their task,
set boundaries around the chosen people at the foot of the
mountain. They wrote down the Torah for the people and
taught them ways of uprightness and judgments of delight,
yet were they punished in anger at the waters of contention.
Let all dwellers on earth know this: if such was the fate of
men flawless in wisdom, what are they who are full of guile
to do. The beloved of the Lord who was first to reign (Saul),
as free from wrong as a year-old child, did not withhold
himself from the war for deliverance. When ordered not to
preserve malicious Amalek, he altered his order and thus had
to blush like a thief, since he had spared fettered Agag, who
sought to steal away through his magic. The Lord repented
