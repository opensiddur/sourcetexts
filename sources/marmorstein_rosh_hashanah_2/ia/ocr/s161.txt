23 Morning Prayer for the New Year 73
Presence. He rose early in the morning and did not delay,
he himself saddled the ass with joy, the same ass which he
(Moses) who drew water (for Jethro’s daughters) also rode
and which the humble one (the Messiah) is due to ride at the
time of redemption. They (Abraham and Isaac) went their
way on a straight road and when they reached Zophim, they
saw a flame rise up. Then did he understand that this was the
mountain for sacrifice. With his finger he pointed out to him
(Isaac) the altar of Him who dwells above — it was the
altar prepared from thé beginning. The only son bade him
bind his hands and feet that he might not rebel against the
commandment of honour to a father so as to profane it.
Abraham bound him on the altar like a lamb for a whole-
offering, he arranged the fire and heaped up the wood. Trem-
bling he took the knife to sacrifice him just as the high priest
offers up the meal-offering mixed with oil. He who is awe-
inspiring in deed, saw how the father bound and the son
offered himself to be bound in trembling. Behold the Erelim
cried out aloud in woe. A voice was heard: do not stretch out
your hand against the lad to make an end of him. The ram
kept for a whole-offering since the six days of creation was
given as his atonement and it was reckoned to him for name
and fame. The Lord swore to bless him with a lofty blessing,
to remember his sacrifice for an outstanding people, to save
them from all guilt and wrong, to acquire them for Himself
as a chosen people and to be our salvation and our help, Selah.
Let this day be recalled before Thee, the purity of the deeds
of the binder and the bound and the residence of the flawless
one (Jacob) in Thy tents. Remember, O Lord, to consider in
compassion. The Tarshishim and glowing Hashmalim kneel
and prostrate themselve before Thee. They revere Thee, one
to another, they sanctify Thee, one to another, and with
threefold sanctification, they sanctify the Holy One.
