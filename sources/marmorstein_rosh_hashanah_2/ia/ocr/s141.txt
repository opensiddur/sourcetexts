Supreme King, He gathers the waters of the sea, excites the
waves of the sea, makes them rage with a mighty
roar so that the world is full of their power, but
He calms them in their onslaught so that they
recede and are no more.
He will reign for ever.
Supreme King, He rules in might, His way is the whirl-wind
and the hurricane, He veils Himself in light to
make night as light as day, thick cloud is His shelter
but light dwells with Him.
He will reign for ever.
Supreme King, clouds are His concealment, flames surround
Him, He rides above the Cherubim, His servants
are flames, planets and stars that increase His
praise. He will reign for ever.
Supreme King, He opens His hand and satisfies, gathers the
waters and allows them to flow and water the dry
land, a third or a quarter of it. One day calls out
to the other to proclaim His praise.
He will reign for ever.
