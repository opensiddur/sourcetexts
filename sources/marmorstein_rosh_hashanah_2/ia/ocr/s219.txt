Him for the abundance of His greatness. Praise Him with
the Shofar blast, praise Him with zither and the harp. Praise
Him with timbrel and dance, praise Him with string instru-
ments and flutes, praise Him with the clash of cymbals, praise
Him with the ring of cymbals. Let all with breath praise the
Lord, Halleluyah. And through Thy servants the prophets it is
written saying: ‘all inhabitants of the world and dwellers on
earth, when the banner is raised on the mountain shall you
see and when the Shofar is blown shall you hear’. And it
was said: ‘And on that day the great Shofar will be blown
and the wanderers in the land of Assyria and the banished in
the land of Egypt shall come and prostrate themselves to the
Lord on the holy mountain in Jerusalem’. And it was said:
‘And the Lord will appear to them and His arrow shall go
forth like lightning and the Lord God will blow the Shofar
and He will walk in the whirlwinds of the south. The Lord
of Hosts will protect them’. So mayest Thou protect Thy
people Israel with Thy peace.
