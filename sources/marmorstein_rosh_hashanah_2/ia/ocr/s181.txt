his hand and took the knife to slaughter his son. Then an
angel of the Lord called to him from the heavens and said:
‘Abraham, Abraham’. He answered, ‘Here I am’. The angel
then said: ‘Do not stretch out your hand against the lad and
do not do anything to him, for now I know that you are
God-fearing since you did not withhold your son, your only
one, from me’. Then Abraham lifted his eyes and saw and
suddenly there was a ram behind, that had been caught by
its horns in the thicket. Abraham went and took the ram and
offered it up as a whole-offering instead of his son. Abraham
called the name of that place ‘the Lord will see’, and it is still
said today ‘on the mountain of the Lord will appear’. And the
angel of the Lord called to Abraham a second time from the
heavens and said: ‘By Myself have I sworn’, says the Lord
‘that because you have done this thing and have not withheld
your son, your only one, I will bless you abundantly and
greatly increase your seed like the stars of the heavens and
like the sand on the shores of the sea and your seed shall take
possession of the gate of its enemies. All the nations of the
earth shall be blessed through your seed as a result of your
listening to My voice’. Abraham returned to his attendants
