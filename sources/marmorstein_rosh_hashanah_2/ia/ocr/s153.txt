Thou didst prepare balm before Thy wound so that they may
turn away from wrong and seek Thy forgiveness. Thou art
close to those that call Thee, to turn back Thy wrath from
them. This day hast Thou chosen to sit in judgment over Thy
creatures. They pass before Thee like lambs for Thy counting.
All of them are scrutinized by the scrutiny of Thy gaze, Thou
dost observe all their hearts together with Thy sight and
decree upon them all Thy decrees. Through Thy speech Thou
dost provide their food and livelihood. Thy people in their
entreaty assemble to implore Thee, blowing Tekiah and
Teruah as it is said in Thy Torah. They confuse the accuser
so that he cannot oppose them before Thee. Thou recallest
in their favour the sacrifice of Isaac and Thine oath. Thou
dost transform the attribute of justice into compassion for
that is Thine attribute and Thou hast mercy on them in Thine
abundant pity for they are Thy children and the flock of Thy
pasture. This day three precise books are opened — that of
the righteous who observe the precepts, that of the middling
and that of the wicked. The righteous are with haste inscribed
for eternal life, the wicked are inscribed for the fiery furnaces
and the middling remain in suspense until the Day of Atone-
ment. If they repent of their deeds and become devout, if
they remedy their actions and improve their steps, then do
they acquire their portion in precious life: but if they are
without merit, it is to death that they are destined. Likewise
shall it be done on the day of judgment. Then will their deeds
