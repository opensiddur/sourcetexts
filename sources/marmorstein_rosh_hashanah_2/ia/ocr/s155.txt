Should they be balanced half and half, then He who is rich
in kindness and inclines towards kindness, will once again
show compassion and suppress the guilt of rebels. He will
forgive guilt and lift up the scale of the deeds of rebellion so
that deeds of loveliness and adornment outweigh them. He
removes sins singly, one by one, for the Lord is righteous in
all His ways and His deeds are kindness. He delights in
repentance more than in whole-offering and regular offering.
Repentance and good deeds act as a shield against spoilers.
O Lord full of mercy, Thou dost rise for judgment over the
creatures of the worlds, do not stir up rage against the
smallest of peoples, who declare the unity of Thy Name by
nights and by days and proclaim Thy triumph constantly in
the evening and in the morning. Like a father on behalf of a
son, let Thy compassion grow warm in consolations, to cleanse
them from the weight of guilt and transgressions. Remember
the covenant with the three flawless ones, first the father of
the multitude who was himself flawless; though tested with
ten powerful ordeals, yet was he found perfect at all these
times. This was the first test: at his birth the astrologers
consulted together and the great ones of the kingdom and the
magicians wished to kill him. Therefore he remained hidden
under the earth for thirteen complete years and did not see
the sun, the moon and the stars of the heights. At the end of
thirteen years he emerged filled with wisdom, rejected the
idols, abominated images and trusted in his Creator so that
his portion fell in loveliness. This was the second test: they
cast him into the furnace of a charcoal fire, whereupon the
King of Glory stretched out His right hand and rescued him
in compassion, saying: 1 am the Lord, who brought you out
of the fire of the Chaldeans. The third test was when the Lord
exiled him from his kindred — an exile in peaceful times.
