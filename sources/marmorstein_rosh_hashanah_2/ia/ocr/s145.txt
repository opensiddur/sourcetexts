65 Morning Prayer for the New Year
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All the mighty on high sing
in praise: The Lord is King.
All who pass through the
worlds confirm with might: The Lord has reigned.
These and those chant in
order: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All who meet in assembly in
uprightness adorn: The Lord is King.
All who are unchangeable,
pay homage in sincerity: The Lord has reigned.
These and those in utterance
pronounce: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All generous with gifts in
speech articulate: The Lord is King.
All honoured with delight
victoriously celebrate: The Lord has reigned.
These and those converse in
conversation: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All those with insight in lofti-
ness esteem: The Lord is King.
All creatures of creation call
out aloud: The Lord has reigned.
These and those murmur in
whispers: The Lord will reign.
The Lord is King, the Lord has reigned,
The Lord will reign for ever and ever.
All fiery angels of the heights
sanctify aloud: The Lord is King.
All leaders of song sing
praises: The Lord has reigned.

These and those sing in har-
