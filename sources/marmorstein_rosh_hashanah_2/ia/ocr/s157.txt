were made impotent. The Archangel Michael descended to
kill him in anger, whereupon he cried out: Wilt thou kill
people though they are innocent, and shed blood. He said to
him: Return the man’s wife, for he is a prophet and the father
of sages. So he took flock and herd, slaves and handmaids,
and gave them to him as an act of appeasement. And Abraham
prayed to God and He responded to him from on high and
healed them from the closing of the womb. The sixth was
when the kings of the East came against him with Kedor-
laomer, King of Elam; those hostile first began with his
brother’s son because of whom they took all Sodom’s wealth.
Then came the fugitive and told him (Abraham) what the
enemies had done. He prepared his trained men, described
as born of his household, who were named Aner, Eshkol,
Mamre and Eliezer. He deployed against them (the enemy)
in the night and smote them with force. That was the night
destined from long ago when he restored all the spoil with
the aid of Him who lays low the proud. The seventh was
when the Lord spoke to him between the divisions and
showed him the four mighty realms that would dominate in
