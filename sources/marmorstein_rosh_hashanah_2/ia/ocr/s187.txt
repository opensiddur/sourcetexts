Israel and Ephraim is my first born. Hear the word of the
Lord, O nations, and tell it in the distant isles and say that
He who has dispersed Israel, will gather it together and
guard it as a shepherd guards his herd. For the Lord has
redeemed Jacob and freed it from a hand stronger than itself.
They shall come and exult on the height of Zion and stream
towards the bounty of the Lord, corn, wine and oil, young
sheep and cattle. Their soul shall be like a well-watered garden
and they will no longer feel anxiety. Then shall the maiden
rejoice in the dance, young men and old together, and I will
turn their mourning into joy and I will comfort them and
give them happiness instead of sorrow. I will make the
priests’ soul overflow with oil and my people shall be satisfied
with my bounty, says the Lord. Thus said the Lord: A voice
is heard in Ramah, lamentation and bitter weeping, it is
Rachel weeping for her children, she refuses to be comforted
because of her children that are no more. Thus said the Lord:
Restrain your voice from weeping and your eyes from tears
for there is a reward for your deeds, says the Lord, and they
shall return from the land of the enemy. There is hope for
your future, says the Lord, and the children shall return to
their boundary. I have clearly heard Ephraim complain: Thou
hast chastised me and I have been chastised like an unyoked
calf, restore me that J may return since Thou art the Lord
my God. For after I had turned away, I repented and after
I understood, I clapped my thigh, I felt ashamed and dis-
