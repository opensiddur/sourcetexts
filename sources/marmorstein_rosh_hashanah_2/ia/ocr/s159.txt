He (Abraham) took and divided them into two divisions in
order to break their strength and annul their memory. The
vulture descended to destroy them, representing David, the
chief of the warriors. At sunrise he waved cloths over them
that the vulture might not rule over them until evening, in
accordance with the secret entrusted to the Sages, in order to
proclaim that they will rule for only one of the days of the
Creator of mountains less two thirds of an hour, — just as
when the sun inclines to the West, the lights are dimmed
two thirds of an hour before sunset. Thus, before evening
comes, light will shine forth for the upright, and in the time
of evening there will be light for those who are honoured.

every year the blood of his circumcision appears like the blood
of the steers and sacrifices and atones for the sins of his people
in order to justify them as if they were upright. The ninth
was when he sent Ishmael and his mother into the wilderness
away from the only son who was offered up on one of the
mountains, both in this world and in the next for the gen-
