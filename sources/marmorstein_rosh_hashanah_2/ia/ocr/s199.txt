(Psalm 24.) By David, a psalm. The earth belongs to the
Lord together with the fullness of it, the world and those who
dwell in it. For He has founded it above the seas and estab-
lished it over rivers. Who may ascend the mountain of the
Lord, and who remain in His holy place? The clean of hands
and the pure of heart, who has not taken my soul for vanity
nor sworn falsely. He will receive blessing from the Lord and
kindness from the God of his salvation. This is the generation
of those that seek Him, that desire Thy countenance, Jacob,
Selah. Lift up your heads, O gates, and lift yourselves up, O
portals of eternity, so that the King of Glory may enter. Who
is this King of Glory? He is the Lord strong and mighty, the
Lord mighty in war. Lift up your heads, O gates, lift up, O
portals of eternity, so that the King of Glory may come. Who
is this King of Glory? The Lord of Hosts, He is the King of
Glory, Selah.
