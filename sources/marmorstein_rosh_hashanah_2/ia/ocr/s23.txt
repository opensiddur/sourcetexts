10 Kiddush for the Festival of the New Year 10
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has created the fruit of the vine.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has chosen us from all peoples and exalted us above all‏
tongues and sanctified us through His commandments. Thou‏
hast given us, O Lord our God, in love this day of Remem-‏
brance, a day of Teruah for a sacred convocation, a reminder‏
of the exodus from Egypt. For Thou hast chosen us and‏
sanctified us from all peoples and Thy word is truth and‏
endures for ever. Blessed art Thou, O Lord, King over all the‏
earth who sanctifies Israel and the Day of Remembrance.
On the termination of the Sabbath:
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has created the lights of fire.‏
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who distinguishes between sacred and profane, between light‏
and darkness, between Israel and the peoples, between the‏
seventh day and the six days of work. Between the sanctity of‏
the Sabbath and the sanctity of the festival hast Thou distin-‏
guished and the seventh day hast Thou sanctified apart from‏
the six days of work. Thou hast distinguished and sanctified‏
Thy people Israel through Thine own holiness. Blessed art‏
Thou, O Lord, who distinguishes between one kind of sanc-‏
tity and another.
Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,‏ ברוך
who has given us life and sustained us and allowed us to‏
reach this time.‏
