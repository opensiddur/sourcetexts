that we may be purified as if by soap; lead us in Thy truth
and secure our future, O forgiver of guilt and pardoner of
transgression to the remnant. When Thou dost ascend the
throne, O Awe-inspiring and Fearful One, to judge this day
the earth’s creatures, in Thy compassion upon us bring forth
for us a ransom, for God do we praise all day. Remove our
evil inclination in Thy pity, be kind to us and shelter us in
the protection of Thy wings, accept our penitence and increase
Thy pardon, pasture Thy people with Thy rod, the flock of
Thine inheritance. Teach us Thy ways that we may stay on
Thy paths, that Thine upright precepts may be constant in
our heart, let Thy compassion be aroused to desire our for-
giveness for we know that we have sinned and none can
stand (innocent). Summon Thy salvation for Thy beloved
bride that thirsts for Thy mercy in longing and desire. Watch
and behold how much she is grieved. Thy righteousness is
like powerful mountains, Thy judgment like the mighty deep.
Take delight in our meditation and the sound of our Teruoth,
let our sins grow pale and free us from our errors, destroy and
crush the crown of our adversaries, be gracious to us O Lord,
be gracious to us, for in Thee does our soul trust. Lead us
near to Thy salvation on this New Year’s day, gather our
dispersed to the fold of my city (Jerusalem) so that enemies
may see that my fame is from Thee. Firm is my hope, the
Lord is my trust.
