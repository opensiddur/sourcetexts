54 Morning Prayer for the New Year 54
Improve your deeds that the covenant may not be‏ שפרו
broken, He who has adorned the skies, will hear your cry,‏
and it will be more pleasing than the sacrifice of oxen to the‏
Lord the Holy One.‏
Let the tribes of those Thou hast called, arise and‏ שבטי
rule, cut off the branches of enemies and let them fall, for‏
both kingdom and ruler belong to the Lord, the Holy One.‏
May our exiled ascend from afar His holy mountain‏ שבותנו
that we may praise Him always in His Inner Sanctuary, for‏
He has remembered His holy word, the Holy One.‏
A glorious tower of strength is the Great Name (of the‏ שבח
Lord) when for eternity He grants might and protection to‏
His appointed king. On that day the great Shofar shall be‏
blown for the Holy One.‏
All inhabitants of the world and dwellers on earth do‏ כל
constantly say: Great things has the Lord performed on earth,‏
and the Lord shall be King over all the earth, the Holy One.‏
Glorification and praise willingly do I bestow, I order‏ אדר
prayer in speech and thought, I call upon the supreme God.‏
On the day chosen from the mighty heavens for upright‏
judgment to glorify His holiness, the Lord rules, the earth‏
rejoices. The world is too small to contain His loftiness, who‏
is there able to describe His might? King of all the earth is‏
God, then sing with understanding.‏
On Sunday
Remove their transgression and atone for their guilt, let their
response be as pleasing as the offering of lambs and steers
with trumpets and the Shofar sound. May their offspring
multiply as the sand without number, their buried ones will
wake and rise from the dust, when a standard is raised on the
mountains, you shall see, and when the Shofar is blown.
Thou wilt rise and let the trumpet sound to destroy‏ תעיר
every evil-doer and Thou art sanctified by those who know
how to blow the Teruah, O Holy One.
