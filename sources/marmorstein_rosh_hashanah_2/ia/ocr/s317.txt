שוכן‎ He who everlasting dwells, exalted and holy is His
Name as it is written: Rejoice, O righteous ones, in the Lord,
the offering of praise is becoming to the upright. Through the
mouth of the upright art Thou exalted and through the words
of the righteous art Thou blessed, and through the tongue of
the pious art Thou sanctified and in the midst of the saints art
Thou extolled.
And through the throngs of the myriads of Thy‏ ובמקהלות
people the House of Israel is Thy Name, O our King, glorified‏
with joy in every generation. For thus it is the duty of all‏
creatures before Thee, O Lord our God and God of our‏
fathers, to thank, to extol, to praise, to glorify, to exalt, to‏
honour, to bless, to elevate and to sing praises with all the‏
words of the songs and eulogies of David the son of Jesse,‏
Thy servant, Thine anointed.‏
May Thy Name be praised for ever, O our King,‏ ישתבח
God the great and holy King in heaven and on earth. For‏
Thine, O Lord our God and God of our fathers, are song and‏
glory, praise and music, might and dominion, victory, great-‏
ness and strength, fame and splendour, blessings and thanks‏
from now until eternity. Blessed art Thou, O Lord God, King‏
famed in eulogies, God of thanksgiving, Masters of wonders,‏
who chooses songs of praise, O King, everlasting God.‏
