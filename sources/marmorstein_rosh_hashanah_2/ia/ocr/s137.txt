composed delightful songs to his Owner, his lofty throne was
founded on grace and he administered judgment and right-

his own son, a slanderer (Tsiba) came to meet him with
falsehood against his master (Mephibosheth) and he (David)
distributed a field (of his master’s property) to him because
of his smooth tongue. But when word of the distribution was
heard in heaven David’s kingdom was divided between his
servant and his grandson. What then can the slanderer do
when he is punished in anger. Communities and myriads and
thousands who have passed away before us, could not justify
themselves that they were free from guilt. Behold, even the
heavens are not pure in His eyes and all the legions on high
are extinguished like a wick, how then can the despicable
and the base find merit. The man who heaps up guile and acts
deceitfully in his affairs, if he says in his heart that none can
testify against him before the Lord, then the beams of his
house will testify, the panelling, the wood and the stone.
O Thou who art pure of eyes, unwilling to see evil, let our
sins sink into the impenetrable depths and on our behalf
perform a sign as a kindness. Though we have strayed from
after Thee, remember the fathers of the world, Thy chosen
ones, and do not hide from the entreaty of those who seek
Thee. In place of steers and a worthy meal-offering, remem-
ber our meditation and grant us clemency, preserve us for
a good life on this beginning of the year.
