390 O King, Thy word is truthful and established from of
old, Thy Name is glorified and established firmly within Thy
congregation, Thy word, O Lord, prevails eternally. O King,
once on this day Thou didst complete the work of Thy
creation (with Adam), then didst Thou save from severe
judgment the man formed in Thine own image, from one
generation to the next does Thy faithfulness prevail. O King,
Thou hast likewise proclaimed freedom to his descendants
that they too may be saved from Thy wrath, O Awe-inspiring
One, when they stand this day before Thy judgment.
