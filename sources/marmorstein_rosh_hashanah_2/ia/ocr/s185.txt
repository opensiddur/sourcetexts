Blessing before the Haphtorah:
ברוך‎ Blessed art Thou, O Lord our God, King of the World,
who has chosen worthy prophets and found pleasure in their
words that are spoken in truth. Blessed art Thou, O Lord,
who has chosen the Torah and His servant Moses, His people
Israel and prophets of truth and righteousness.
Haphtarah
(Jeremiah XXXI, 1—19)
n> Thus said the Lord: There did find favour in the wilder-
ness a people of survivors from the sword, Israel on its way
to find rest. From the distance did the Lord appear to me:
With eternal love did I love you, therefore shall I lead you
with kindness. I will yet build you up and you shall be built
up, O maid of Israel, you shall yet beat your drums and go
forth in playful dance. You shall yet plant vineyards on the
mountains of Samaria where the planters once planted and
praised. For there was a day on which the watchmen on
Mount Ephraim called out: Arise, let us go up to Zion, to
the Lord our God. For thus says the Lord: exult with joy for
Jacob’s sake and triumph at the head of the nations, proclaim,
praise and say: The Lord has saved Thy people, the remnant
of Israel. Behold I will bring them from the land of the north
and gather them from the corners of the earth, blind and
lame among them, the woman with child together with one
who has just given birth, a great assembly shall return here.
