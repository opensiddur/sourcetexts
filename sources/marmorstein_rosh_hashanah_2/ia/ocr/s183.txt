The second scroll of the Torah is then placed next to the first on the
reading desk and the Reader says half-kaddish. The first scroll is then
raised and rolled together. After that, the second scroll is unrolled and
someone is called to the reading for Maphtir which is taken from
Pinhas.

After the reading, the Torah scroll is lifted up and rolled
