There is none like Thee, O Lord, Thou art great and Thy
Name is as great as Thy greatness. Thine are the heavens and
all their hosts were created to serve Thee. Thine is the earth
and its fullness is Thy footstool. Thine is the sea and the dry
land and Thy fingers have established everything. Thine is
silver and gold and all Thy precious objects, Thine are all
souls together stored in Thy treasure troves. Thine is wisdom
and insight and reason is but part of Thine understanding.
O wise of heart and strong in power, grant guidance in Thy
love, for who in heaven compares to Thee to resemble Thee.
Who is like Thee, mighty God, a sign among Thy hosts. O
God who is revered in the great counsel of the saints and held
in awe above all.Thy surroundings, darkness is Thy shelter
and thick cloud Thy tent. From the lustre in front of Thee
there is hail and the fire of Thy gle ving coal. O faithful God
who keeps the covenant, grace is in Thy keeping, to give each
according to his ways when Thou payest out Thy wage. As
for the Rock, flawless is His work and without fault, Thine
action is truth, Thy judgments are like the great deep and
as the mighty mountains is Thy righteousness. Thou art helper
and supporter of all the eager in Thy work. Everything hast
Thou created beautifully at its right time according to Thy
wisdom. Before mountains were born and Thine earth was in
labour, Thou didst create repentance as a healing remedy.
While there was yet no wound, Thou didst anticipate it with
Thy cure, before the disease Thou didst make Thy healing
apparent. For it is revealed and observed before the throne
of Thy Presence that no man is too righteous on earth to
beware of Thy glowing coal, no man who does only good and
departs not from Thy Torah. For Thou dost not rely on those
who serve Thee and on those who minister to Thee, but Thou
